Security

Your proposals are safe. Here's exactly how.

I know what's in a federal proposal. Pricing, teaming partners, technical approach — you can't afford a leak. Here's how ProposalPulse and MarketPulse protect your documents.

Effective March 17, 2026 · Mission Meets Tech LLC · North Carolina

What Happens When You Upload a Proposal

  1. 1. Document travels over TLS 1.2+ to our hosting provider's SOC 2 infrastructure.
  2. 2. We extract text. Original file is not stored.
  3. 3. Text sent to our AI analysis provider. Their policy: inputs never used to train models.
  4. 4. Scorecard stored in our database provider on cloud infrastructure, AES-256 encrypted at rest.
  5. 5. After 90 days, document text permanently purged. Scorecards retained.

What We Do NOT Do

  • ‣ Don't sell, share, or license your data
  • ‣ Don't use proposals to train any AI model
  • ‣ Don't store original uploaded files
  • ‣ Don't allow cross-user data access (email-scoped)
  • ‣ Don't process classified, ITAR, or export-restricted material

Encryption

In Transit

TLS 1.2+ on all connections between your browser, our servers, and third-party APIs.

At Rest

AES-256 encryption on all database storage (cloud infrastructure).

Payment

Our payment processor handles all payment processing. We never see or store card numbers.

Access Controls

Cryptographic HMAC token per scoring request. No guessable URLs. Server-side functions only — no client-side database access.

Third-Party Services

AI Analysis Proposal analysis — No training on API inputs
Research Intel MarketPulse research — No training on API inputs
Database Cloud infrastructure — SOC 2, encrypted
Hosting Hosting / functions — SOC 2
Payments Payment processing — PCI DSS Level 1
Email Email delivery — SOC 2

Your Rights

  • ‣ Delete your data: email mary@missionmeetstech.com and we'll remove everything within 30 days
  • ‣ Export your scorecards: request a copy of all your assessment results
  • ‣ Ask anything: questions about how we handle your data are always welcome

What Happens When You Request a MarketPulse Brief

  1. 1. Your research question travels over TLS 1.2+ to our hosting provider's SOC 2 infrastructure.
  2. 2. Query sent to our AI analysis provider and our research intelligence provider. Neither trains on API inputs.
  3. 3. Generated report stored in our database provider on cloud infrastructure, AES-256 encrypted at rest.
  4. 4. Report PDF delivered to your email via our email delivery provider (SOC 2).
  5. 5. Query text retained for quality assurance. Reports retained while account active.

What We Do NOT Do with MarketPulse Data

  • ‣ Don't share your research questions with other customers
  • ‣ Don't use your queries to train any AI model
  • ‣ Don't sell or license your data

Who We Are

Mission Meets Tech LLC, a North Carolina LLC, founder-led. Built for federal contractors who can't afford to get data security wrong.
