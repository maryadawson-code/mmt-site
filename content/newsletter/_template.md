Lead with a real moment. Mary's voice — warm but fierce, story-first, conversational. Use "I" and "my" freely. Fragments are fine. Start sentences with And or But.

Example opener: "I was on a call last week with a procurement officer who said the quiet part out loud. She told me — and I am going to say this like I heard it — ..."

Use the contrast engine often. The military can synchronize a kill chain in milliseconds but cannot move a veteran's health record across two government systems.
