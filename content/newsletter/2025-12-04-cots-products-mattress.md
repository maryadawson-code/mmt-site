After the Great Depression, people kept money in the mattress. Some defense health IT vendors are doing the same with their COTS products.

There's a pattern in defense health IT acquisition that doesn't get talked about enough: vendors sitting on commercial products rather than investing in modernization. The implications for the government — and ultimately for service members — are significant.
