The May Capture Intelligence Sheet led with **VA Enterprise Imaging** because
that's the procurement signal flashing brightest right now in the VA forecast.
It's worth restating in this format: VA OIT's quarterly forecast refresh added
the Enterprise Imaging line item ahead of a draft RFP that we expect inside the
next two quarters. If you're not already engaged with VA OIT on imaging
infrastructure or PACS modernization, the window to position is narrowing.

**DHA forecast ripples from the April 2026 reorg.** The DHA reorg
restructured assistant-director portfolios, which means the official DHA
forecast as published is structurally aligned to the OLD org chart. PEOs
roll up differently in the new structure. Until the next forecast refresh
catches up, cross-reference the forecast directly with the new IOC/FOC
PMO map (covered in the April 29 Capture Corner). Forecast addenda
posted between full refreshes are the place to watch.

**ARPA-H Resilient Systems pipeline thickening.** The ARPA-H people
directory shows seven program managers in Resilient Systems, several of
whom have moved into program-launch posture. ARPA-H doesn't post a
traditional forecast — its program announcements drop on
[arpa-h.gov/research-funding](https://arpa-h.gov/research-funding) without
the long-runway notice agencies like VA give you. If your capture pipeline
includes ARPA-H, set up a recurring check on the program announcements
page rather than relying on the procurement-forecast hub.

**The methodology note for subscribers:** federal procurement forecasts
are not a single source of truth. They're per-agency snapshots, updated on
their own cadence, in formats that range from a downloadable Excel sheet
(VA, GSA) to a search interface (HHS via mySBCX) to plain HTML
(DHA-via-DoD). The MMT Forecast Delta Tracker layers editorial commentary
on top of the official sources we link from the
[Forecast Portals page](/premium/forecast-delta.html) — because the
diff matters more than the snapshot, and nobody else is publishing it
month-over-month.
