## Top 3 Signals to Act On Before Monday

### 1. The DHP budget split is now public — re-anchor your FY2027 narrative to COMP, not "DHP."
The FY2027 request formally splits the Defense Health Program into the **Combat Operational and Medical Readiness account (COMP, ~$20.3B)** and the **Private Sector Care Program (PSCP, ~$22.2B)** ([Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/pay-benefits/military-benefits/health-care/2026/05/05/defense-department-proposes-splitting-military-health-system-budget/)). Every defense health IT obligation — IMIT, JOMIS, EIDS, base comms — lives in COMP. PSCP is the TRICARE checkbook.
**Do this before Monday:** Scrub any pipeline narrative or capability statement that still says "Defense Health Program funds X." Map your pursuit to a COMP line. Anything tied to PSCP is the wrong account and reads as a sourcing error to a knowledgeable evaluator.

### 2. DHA's voice/dictation demand signal + ambient-listening rollout = a named enterprise AI-clinical buy taking shape.
DHA's **Enterprise Voice Dictation System RFI** (DRD-26-P-034671) closed Feb 23, but it pairs with the now-confirmed phased **ambient-listening** rollout — auto-drafting clinical notes into the EHR, expanding to all military hospitals "in the coming months" ([DHA.mil](https://dha.mil/News/2026/05/19/15/01/DefenHealth-Network-Central-rolls-out-ambient-listening-technology)). The requirement and the operational reality are converging toward a follow-on acquisition.
**Do this before Monday:** If you have ambient/voice, NLP-to-EHR, or clinical-documentation capability, the RFI window is past — so go direct. Build the white paper, request a capabilities briefing with the DHA program office, and position with OWHA as requirement owner *before* the follow-on solicitation posts. The team that engaged the January RFI has a head start; close that gap now.

### 3. Chillicothe goes live on the federal EHR Saturday (June 6) — the VA deployment clock is moving again.
Chillicothe VAMC deploys the federal EHR June 6 ([reporting](https://www.facebook.com/thetelegramnews/posts/1607250048068332/)). Each go-live restarts the wave of site-support, change-management, training, and integration task-order demand that historically trails a deployment by 30–90 days.
**Do this before Monday:** If you sell deployment support, training, or post-go-live sustainment to VA, flag Chillicothe and the next sites now. Watch T4NG2 — currently quiet — for the trailing support task orders. Be positioned with the holder before the RFQ drops.
