We Are a Combat Support Agency
We deliver joint warfighting capabilities—medical logistics, IT infrastructure, trauma systems, research, pharmacy, and blood support—that directly affect survivability and lethality. Our integrated system ensures every warrior wearing the cloth of our Nation is medically ready to answer the call at a moment’s notice.
