Key Leaders
Director
Mark Cruz , [PDF - 149 KB], a citizen of the Klamath Tribes, currently leads as the 12th director of the Indian Health Service (IHS), an agency within the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS).
Deputy Director
Benjamin Smith , [PDF - 157 KB], an enrolled member of the Navajo Nation, is the deputy director for the Indian Health Service.
Chief of Staff
Clayton W. Fulton, [PDF - 146 KB], an enrolled member of the Cherokee Nation, is the chief of staff for the Indian Health Service.
Deputy Director for Management Operations
Darrell LaRoche, [PDF - 169 KB] , an enrolled member of the Lower Brule Sioux Tribe in South Dakota, is the deputy director for management operations for the Indian Health Service.
Chief Medical Officer
Loretta Christensen, M.D. , [PDF - 167 KB] an enrolled member of the Navajo Nation, serves as the Chief Medical Officer for the Indian Health Service.
Deputy Director for Intergovernmental and External Affairs
Stacey Ecoffey , [PDF - 128 KB] an enrolled member of the Oglala Sioux Tribe, is the deputy director for intergovernmental and external affairs for the Indian Health Service.
Chief Information Officer
Mitchell Thornbrugh, [PDF - 205 KB], an enrolled member of the Muscogee Creek Nation, is the chief information officer and director of the Office of Information Technology for the Indian Health Service.
Deputy Director for Quality Healthcare and Enterprise Risk Management
Michelle Boylan, [PDF - 173 KB] , is the deputy director for quality healthcare and risk management for the Indian Health Service.
Deputy Director for Field Operations
Beverly Cotton, [PDF - 131 KB] , an enrolled member of the Mississippi Band of Choctaw Indians, is the deputy director for field operations of the Indian Health Service.
Deputy Director for Field Operations
Rear Adm. Kelly Battese, [PDF - 267 KB] , an enrolled member of the Miami Tribe of Oklahoma, is the deputy director for field operations, Southern Tier, at the Indian Health Service (IHS).
Acting Deputy Director for Field Operations
Capt. Joe Bryant, [PDF - 187 KB] , a citizen of the Cherokee Nation, serves as the acting deputy director for field operations for the Indian Health Service (IHS).
Director of Strategic Initiatives
Kim Hartwig, [PDF - 151 KB] , a Nimiipuu (Nez Perce) board certified family physician, recently joined the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services as director of strategic initiatives for the Indian Health Service.
