How we source this data

Verified

Contract names, agencies, award dates, and ceiling values confirmed against SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, agency announcements, or congressional records. Each entry shows a "last verified" date.

Estimated

Recompete timelines, contract ceilings without public disclosure, and opportunity confidence scores are estimates based on historical patterns and available signals. These are labeled as estimates wherever they appear.

Inferred

Strategic implications, likely vehicle assignments, and competitive positioning are editorial analysis based on procurement patterns. These represent informed judgment, not confirmed facts.

Curated contract data is reviewed and updated regularly. Opportunity Radar and Vehicle Radar run automated daily scans. Always verify independently on SAM.gov or the relevant agency procurement portal before making business decisions.
