Upload your proposal document. Get a scored assessment across nine criteria in under 60 seconds.

Supports pitch decks, white papers, RFP responses, capabilities statements, pricing volumes, and executive summaries.

What are the 9 scoring criteria?

Criteria adapt to your document type. Here's what we evaluate:

  1. Problem Clarity — Is the agency's mission problem specific and urgent?
  2. Mission Relevance — Does the solution connect to stated mission outcomes?
  3. Solution / Technical Approach — Is the methodology clear and feasible?
  4. Market Sizing / Scope — Are contract vehicles and federal market addressed?
  5. Team & Credibility — Relevant credentials, clearances, federal experience?
  6. Traction / Past Performance — Real pilots, contracts, or references?
  7. Financials / Pricing — Numbers defensible? Unit economics clear?
  8. Competitive Position — Differentiation specific? Incumbent risk addressed?
  9. Writing Quality / The Ask — Clear, organized, and actionable?
