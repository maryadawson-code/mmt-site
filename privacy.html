Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Mission Meets Tech handles your data. Short version: we respect it.

Effective March 17, 2026 · Mission Meets Tech LLC · North Carolina

Who We Are

Mission Meets Tech LLC ("MMT," "we," "us") operates missionmeetstech.com and the MissionPulse platform. We provide federal health IT intelligence, including the MMT newsletter, Mission Meets Reality podcast, ProposalPulse document analysis, and MarketPulse tactical briefs.

Information We Collect

We collect only what we need to deliver our services:

  • ‣ Email address — when you subscribe to the newsletter, use ProposalPulse, or purchase a MarketPulse brief
  • ‣ Documents you upload — proposal documents submitted to ProposalPulse for analysis
  • ‣ Payment information — processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number
  • ‣ Usage data — anonymous page views via Plausible Analytics (no cookies, no tracking)

Website Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-respecting analytics tool. No cookies, no personal data collection, no cross-site tracking. We see aggregate page views and referrers. We don't see who you are.

Email Subscriptions

Newsletter subscriptions are managed through Buttondown and LinkedIn. We collect your email address to send you the newsletter. That's it. You can unsubscribe at any time from any email we send. We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties.

ProposalPulse — Document Analysis

When you submit a document to ProposalPulse, here is exactly what happens:

  • ‣ Your document is uploaded to our server (Netlify Functions) for text extraction
  • ‣ Extracted text is sent to the Anthropic API (Claude) for analysis
  • ‣ Anthropic does not train on API inputs. Your content is not used to improve AI models.
  • ‣ Scorecards are stored in our database (Supabase, hosted on AWS) for retrieval and email delivery
  • ‣ Extracted document text is retained for up to 90 days to enable the Red Team Review, then purged
  • ‣ Your content is not shared with any third party beyond Anthropic's API processing

Important

Do not submit classified, export-controlled (ITAR/EAR), or government-restricted information to ProposalPulse. This service is designed for unclassified proposal drafts only.

MarketPulse — Tactical Briefs

MarketPulse tactical briefs are generated using publicly available federal procurement data. Your email is collected for delivery and stored in our database. Brief content is generated via the Anthropic API and Perplexity API (for web research). The same no-training guarantee applies: neither provider trains on your data.

Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID to manage your account. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card number. Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified.

Data Storage & Security

  • ‣ In transit: All connections use TLS/HTTPS encryption
  • ‣ At rest: Database storage is encrypted (Supabase on AWS with AES-256)
  • ‣ Access: Database access is restricted to server-side functions via service keys. No client-side access.
  • ‣ Retention: Uploaded document text is purged after 90 days. Scorecards and account data are retained while your account is active.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate MMT. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • ‣ Anthropic — AI analysis (API, no training on inputs)
  • ‣ Supabase — database (hosted on AWS)
  • ‣ Stripe — payment processing
  • ‣ Netlify — hosting and serverless functions
  • ‣ Resend — transactional email delivery
  • ‣ Buttondown — newsletter subscriptions
  • ‣ Plausible — privacy-friendly analytics (no cookies)

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us. We will remove your account, scorecards, and any stored document text within 30 days. Newsletter unsubscribe is instant via any email footer link.

Changes to This Policy

We'll update this page when our practices change. Material changes will be noted with a new effective date at the top. We won't reduce your rights without notice.

Questions

If you have questions about how we handle your data, email mary@missionmeetstech.com.
