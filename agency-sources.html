No vendor sites. No paywalls. The primary .gov and .mil sources that drive federal health IT decisions.

Defense Health

DHA, FEHRM, TRICARE, and military medical research. The agencies that run health IT for 9.6 million beneficiaries.

DHA Main ↗

health.mil · Portal

Defense Health Agency official site. News, leadership, policy documents.

Why it matters: Ground truth for anything DHA announces or funds.

DHA Contracting ↗

health.mil · Procurement

DHACA contract opportunities and procurement guidance.

Why it matters: Where DHA health IT solicitations originate.

OPMED (formerly PEO DHMS) ↗

health.mil · Portal

Medical IT development, acquisition programs, MHS GENESIS.

Why it matters: The office that buys and deploys MHS GENESIS.

FEHRM ↗

fehrm.gov · Policy

Federal EHR Modernization. Joint DoD-VA interoperability.

Why it matters: The office that coordinates the joint DoD-VA EHR.

TRICARE ↗

tricare.mil · Portal

Beneficiary portal, provider info, regional contract details.

Why it matters: Managed care contracts for 9.6M beneficiaries.

CDMRP ↗

cdmrp.health.mil · Research

Military medical research funding opportunities.

Why it matters: Billions in directed research funding for health tech.

VA Health IT

Department of Veterans Affairs. The largest integrated health system in the U.S., serving 9 million enrolled Veterans.

VA Main ↗

va.gov · Portal

Department of Veterans Affairs official site.

Why it matters: Central hub for all VA programs and announcements.

VHA ↗

va.gov · Portal

Veterans Health Administration. 1,300+ facilities.

Why it matters: Runs the largest federal health system in the country.

VA OIT ↗

digital.va.gov · Policy

VA Office of Information and Technology. IT strategy and programs.

Why it matters: Sets the IT strategy for every VA facility.

VA EHR Modernization ↗

digital.va.gov · Policy

EHRM program status, deployment schedule, resources.

Why it matters: Tracks the most-watched IT program in federal health.

VA Lighthouse APIs ↗

developer.va.gov · Data / APIs

VA's public API platform. Health, benefits, and facilities data.

Why it matters: The open data layer for building on VA systems.

Connected Care ↗

connectedcare.va.gov · Portal

Telehealth, remote monitoring, My HealtheVet.

Why it matters: VA leads federal telehealth adoption.

VA Open Data ↗

data.va.gov · Data / APIs

Open datasets for research and analysis.

Why it matters: Raw data behind VA performance and outcomes.

VA OIG ↗

vaoig.gov · Research

Inspector General. Audits, investigations, EHR deployment oversight.

Why it matters: Independent audits that drive VA IT course corrections.

Interoperability and Standards

ONC, FHIR, TEFCA, and the frameworks that govern how health data moves between systems.

ONC/ASTP ↗

healthit.gov · Policy

Office of the National Coordinator. Health IT policy and certification.

Why it matters: Sets the rules for health IT certification nationwide.

TEFCA ↗

healthit.gov · Policy

Trusted Exchange Framework. National health data exchange infrastructure.

Why it matters: The nationwide framework for cross-network data sharing.

HL7 FHIR ↗

hl7.org · Policy

FHIR specification. The modern healthcare interoperability standard.

Why it matters: The API standard every federal health system is adopting.

ONC Certification ↗

healthit.gov · Policy

Health IT certification program details and requirements.

Why it matters: Determines which EHR products can sell to the feds.

eHealth Exchange ↗

ehealthexchange.org · Portal

Largest health information network in the U.S.

Why it matters: Where DoD and VA actually exchange records today.

Carequality ↗

carequality.org · Portal

Interoperability framework connecting health networks nationwide.

Why it matters: The trust framework behind cross-network queries.

Acquisition and Contracting

Where federal contracts are posted, tracked, and awarded. Registration, vehicles, and procurement guidance.

SAM.gov ↗

sam.gov · Procurement

Entity registration, contract opportunities, exclusions.

Why it matters: Where every federal opportunity gets posted.

SAM.gov Award Data ↗

sam.gov · Data / APIs

Historical contract award data and FPDS search.

Why it matters: Research who won what, for how much, and when.

USAspending ↗

usaspending.gov · Data / APIs

Federal spending transparency. Search by agency, contract, recipient.

Why it matters: Follow the money on any federal health IT contract.

NITAAC ↗

nitaac.nih.gov · Procurement

NIH IT Acquisition and Assessment Center. CIO-SP3 and CIO-CS vehicles.

Why it matters: Major IT GWAC vehicles for health agencies.

GSA eBuy ↗

ebuy.gsa.gov · Procurement

GSA's online Request for Quote system for Schedule and GWAC purchases.

Why it matters: Task order RFQs under GSA Schedule IT contracts.

DAU ↗

dau.edu · Research

Defense Acquisition University. Training and certification resources.

Why it matters: If you want to understand how DoD buys, start here.

AcqNotes ↗

acqnotes.com · Research

Community reference for defense acquisition processes and terminology.

Why it matters: Plain-English reference when FAR jargon gets thick.

Federal budget justifications, spending data, and analysis for defense health and VA programs.

USAspending.gov ↗

usaspending.gov · Data / APIs

Track federal spending by agency, contract, and category.

Why it matters: The definitive source for where federal dollars go.

DoD Comptroller ↗

comptroller.defense.gov · Data / APIs

Defense budget materials, justification documents.

Why it matters: The line-item breakdown of every DoD program.

DHP Budget ↗

comptroller.defense.gov · Data / APIs

Defense Health Program budget justification (search for DHP).

Why it matters: Shows exactly how much DHA plans to spend on IT.

VA Budget ↗

department.va.gov · Data / APIs

VA budget submission and justification documents.

Why it matters: VA IT spending trends start in these documents.

CBO Military Health ↗

cbo.gov · Research

Congressional Budget Office analysis of military health spending.

Why it matters: Independent cost projections Congress relies on.

gao.gov · Research

Government Accountability Office reports on military and VA health.

Why it matters: The reports that trigger congressional hearings.

CRS Reports ↗

congress.gov · Research

Congressional Research Service. Authoritative policy analysis.

Why it matters: The background briefings that inform legislation.

RAND Military Health ↗

rand.org · Research

RAND Corporation research on military health policy and systems.

MITRE Health FFRDC ↗

CMS Alliance to Modernize Healthcare (Health FFRDC).

Why it matters: Independent technical advice to CMS and HHS.

DoD IG ↗

Department of Defense Inspector General. Audits and evaluations.

VA OIG ↗

vaoig.gov · Research

VA Inspector General. Audits and investigations.

Why it matters: The EHR deployment oversight everyone reads.

Cybersecurity

FedRAMP, CMMC, NIST frameworks, and the security baselines that govern federal health IT systems.

FedRAMP Marketplace ↗

marketplace.fedramp.gov · Portal

Search FedRAMP-authorized cloud products.

Why it matters: If it is not FedRAMP-authorized, it is not in the cloud.

CMMC (DoD CIO) ↗

dodcio.defense.gov · Policy

Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification program details.

Why it matters: Required for every DoD contractor handling CUI.

NIST SP 800-171 ↗

csrc.nist.gov · Policy

CUI protection requirements for non-federal systems.

Why it matters: The controls CMMC is built on.

NIST SP 800-53 ↗

csrc.nist.gov · Policy

Security and privacy controls catalog.

Why it matters: The master control set for federal information systems.

DoD Zero Trust Strategy ↗

dodcio.defense.gov · Policy

DoD's zero trust architecture implementation plan.

Why it matters: Every DoD network must hit zero trust targets by FY27.

DISA STIGs ↗

cyber.mil · Portal

Security Technical Implementation Guides.

Why it matters: The hardening checklists every DoD system must pass.
