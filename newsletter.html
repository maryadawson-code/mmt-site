What to Expect

Policy Analysis

Each issue breaks down federal health IT policy changes and explains what they mean for the people who have to implement them. No jargon walls. No filler. Just the operational implications.

Budget Intelligence

Defense health appropriations, contract vehicle updates, and the budget signals that indicate where the market is heading. Translated from budget-speak into actionable context.

Market Context

DHA modernization, VA health IT strategy, TRICARE reform, and the acquisition dynamics that create opportunity — or risk — for contractors and program managers.

Practitioner Perspective

Written by someone who has worked in this space since 2007. Every issue is grounded in firsthand experience with the systems, agencies, and stakeholders involved.

Who This Is For

Mission Meets Tech is written for defense contractors, government program managers, health IT leaders, and technology strategists who need to understand the federal health landscape without reading 200-page policy documents themselves.

If your work touches DHA, VA, MHS, TRICARE, or defense health acquisition, this newsletter gives you the context you need to make better decisions faster.
