IHS has a confirmed Director again. Mark Cruz (Klamath Tribes) was Senate-confirmed August 7, 2026 as the 12th IHS Director, ending roughly 18 months without one. The bench under him is thin: 6 of 12 Area Director seats are Acting, two headquarters offices are Vacant, and the DAP staff page shows multiple open contracting officer positions. For contractors, the load-bearing facts are lower down: CIO Mitchell Thornbrugh owns the PATH EHR modernization (GDIT integrator, Oracle Health EHR, Lawton pilot go-live 2026), and Chantel Smith remains the permanent Head of Contracting Activity.
