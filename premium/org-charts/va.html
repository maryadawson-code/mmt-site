RISE Initiative (Restructure for Impact & Sustainability Effort)
Announced Dec 15, 2025. Implementation: 2026 to 2027.

Key Structural Changes:
• 18 VISNs → 5 regions, supported by 15 Health Service Areas (HSAs), reporting directly to USH
• VHA COO position eliminated
• VHACO staff placed under USH, DUSH, or Associate DUSH
• New Medical Operations Center delivers consistent guidance to facilities
• Medical facility directors empowered with more local authority
• No RIF · staffing at VAMCs and clinics unchanged

Timeline: Leadership consolidation begins mid-2026; VISN realignment starts late 2026 and completes around mid-2027 (18 to 24 months). Projected cost at least $312M.
Goal: Eliminate redundant bureaucracy, clearer accountability chain Secretary → front-line clinical leaders.

FEHRM Federal EHR Modernization · VA / DoD Joint Program

The Federal EHR Modernization (FEHRM) is jointly managed by VA and DoD (DHA). FEHRM Director Dr. Leslie Sofocleous (per the Aug 25, 2026 org chart monitor; succeeded Bill Tinston) coordinates Oracle Cerner deployment across both enterprises. As of April 2026, VA has 13+ active deployment sites. Deputy Secretary Lawrence personally oversees the EHRM program for VA, appearing weekly on the “Federal Electronic Health Record Update” internal program.

⚠ Risk Flag: The DHA April 2026 reorg · PAE “Construct Pending” status and HCDS recompete · creates a governance gap for FEHRM coordination on the DoD side during the IOC to FOC transition (target: Jul 19, 2026). On the VA side, the vacant CIO position (Gary Shatswell pending confirmation) adds further coordination risk for the joint program.
