How it's scored

Each market dimension is worth 25 points, scored from direct API queries. The verdict layers your company profile on top: PURSUE · DEFEND (incumbent) · QUALIFY · MONITOR · CAPTURE VALIDATION · NO-BID. Without a profile, scoring is a generic preliminary read and the engine refuses to declare a hard NO-BID.
