The lead finding for a capture team: the campaign plan gives currency hard numbers and gives the workforce dated tasks with no number, and its sequencing puts reservists ahead of contractors. The Core 15 plan their staffing "based on current authorizations," a reserve-component business case comes before contracting options, and the crisis staffing work sits in LOE 3 with an end date of September 30, 2027. Fiscal 2027 pays for uniforms through the services, pays to retain the civilians DHA already has, and holds contractor headcount flat while cutting contract dollars. The near-term market is analytics, workforce systems, hiring throughput, and VA partnership operations. Contract backfill is the fallback the plan is written to reach last.

Source: USAJOBS historic announcement data for series 0620. Counts are announcements; they do not measure hires or incumbents. [CC3]

Every one of the 486 GS-5 announcements in 2025 and 2026 lists promotion potential as "None," while the rare DHA posting with a ladder reads "6." [CC3][CC10] The duties did not shrink: a 2026 GS-5 posting at Walter Reed requires IV fluid and medication experience and lists sterile field setup, the same work a 2024 GS-6 posting at Lyster covered. [CC11][CC12] DHA is posting in volume at the new ceiling, with 376 announcements through October 9 against 195 in all of 2025. [CC3] The March 31 position description standardization is the first plan task that could change the ceiling. [CC1]

My read for clinical support vendors is a split market. Where a hospital can fill civil service LPN jobs at GS-5, the contract clinical support line shrinks on schedule. Where it cannot, the contracted LPN remains the backstop, and the $294.3 million cut becomes harder to execute. Womack Army Medical Center posted 19 GS-5 LPN announcements between January 2025 and October 7, 2026. [CC3][CC13] Repeat postings at a single site can mean multiple vacancies, turnover, or positions that did not fill; the announcement data cannot tell them apart, so treat repost frequency as a signal to investigate.

The qualification rule compounds it. Under OPM's nurse standard, an associate-degree RN needs a full year of RN experience to qualify for any GS grade, and LPN experience does not count. [CC14] The FY2025 NDAA's Section 716 waiver covers bachelor's-degree nurses only; an associate-degree version proposed in a Senate amendment did not survive conference. [CC7] Contract employers can hire the same nurse the week the license arrives.

D. Path A is the default through FY27 because it is the request on the table and the plan builds staffing on current authorizations. [CC2][CC1] Path B is the one the public record argues for most loudly: GAO documented DHA's FY2025 inability to hire civilians, and the Inspector General found clinics below DHA's own model of two clinical support staff per provider. [CC15][CC16] Path D is the one the plan's sequencing points to. When Madigan Army Medical Center had 462 soldiers ordered to deploy under PROFIS in 2009, it covered four deployed nurse anesthetists with six reservist anesthetists on 90-day rotations. [CC17] RAND's 2013 evaluation recorded the cost from the inside: medical personnel perceived that PROFIS deployments increased workload and reduced access at the hospitals left behind. [CC18]

6. Where the demand sits

Confidence: High on the cited budget, report, and plan language. Analytical on the demand map.

Workforce and readiness systems. ARMOR, the plan's Assessment of Readiness and Mission Operational Resources requirement, gets a governance cell in October, a timekeeping and scheduling capability to retire DMHRSi in March, the EMQAS credentialing capability to retire CCQAS in June, and proficiency predictive modeling in September 2027, all under PAE Medical Digital Solutions. [CC1] Outside workload capture by January 30 and partner proficiency data streams by August 31 feed the same picture. [CC1] GAO found 39 percent of personnel with partnership work on readiness dashboards had no partnership hours in the timecard system, which is the baseline problem these tasks exist to fix. [CC19]

Hiring throughput. DHA's 2025 nursing staffing report measured 115 days from tentative offer to start and named four initiatives to shorten it, and the plan adds a security check model inside the hiring action by March 31. [CC4][CC1] Work that executes DHA's own named initiatives grows under every path.

Operating room and acuity analytics. Perioperative standards in March, acuity modeling in March, an operating room dashboard in May, and quarterly reporting of staffed rooms, block utilization, throughput, surgical wait, recapture, and proficiency from June 30 make LOE 2 the most dashboard-dense line in the plan. [CC1]

VA partnership operations. Agreements on authorization and on DoW providers at VA medical centers by December 31, data sharing guidance by March 31, hospital-level targets by May 30, veteran access policy by July 30, and a simplified authorization process by August 30, 2027 add up to an operating workstream with no named contractor. [CC1]

Staffing models and ratios. The Inspector General found clinics below the family medicine model finalized July 2, 2025, and the plan's manpower alignment by December 31 runs against manning documents. [CC16][CC1] Ratio measurement by clinic and support-staff gap analysis have an IG finding and a plan task behind them.

What to avoid. Retiree volume does not create In-House Care savings, because MERHCF pays for it, and a business case that claims otherwise will fail review. [CC9] Pitches that assume a civilian hiring surge in FY27 will fail too; the plan builds on current authorizations and the request funds none. [CC2][CC1]

7. The counterargument, stated fairly

Confidence: This is the strongest case against the reads above, and parts of it are right.

The plan does address backfill. Surge staffing requirements, alternate crisis staffing solutions, and the reserve business case are real tasks with dates. The missing number may reflect sequencing, with requirements first and targets later, and the May hospital-level targets could carry one.

The GS-5 ceiling may be working. DHA posted 376 LPN announcements in the first nine months of 2026, nearly double all of 2025. [CC3] If those positions fill, the civil service is absorbing clinical support at a lower grade and the contract line shrinks on schedule. Fill rates and time-to-fill would settle it; the posting data cannot.

Uniformed growth is the strategy. LOE 2 measures success in military clinicians who meet their wartime standards, and civilians do not count toward it. [CC1] A budget that grows uniforms is executing the plan, and contractors who sell against that direction are selling against the Director's line of effort.

Contract dollars can fall without capacity loss. Contractor FTEs hold flat while dollars fall, which is consistent with consolidation and repricing of administrative work more than loss of clinical support. [CC2] The October 2 brief rated administrative functions as the highest exposure for that reason. [CC9]

8. What to do in the next ninety days

Confidence: Analytical.

  1. Re-sort your MTF clinical support portfolio by labor category and site. For each LPN, medical assistant, and technician position you hold, note whether the site's civil service postings for the same series stop at GS-5. Sites with repeat GS-5 postings are where your contracted staff are hardest to replace and where your option position is strongest.

  2. Position for March 31. Bring reserve credentialing, scheduling, and readiness support to the reserve business case, and price contract backfill as the fallback the plan describes. [CC1]

  3. Build a hiring-throughput offer around DHA's named initiatives. Price pre-employment processing and onboarding as a fixed-fee service measured in days from tentative offer to start, against the 115-day baseline, and include the security check model due in March. [CC4][CC1]

  4. Write the deployment-day white paper now, aimed at April and September. Model a Core 15-sized hospital losing one in five uniformed staff, by specialty, against the two-per-provider support model, using Madigan 2009 and the RAND evaluation as precedent. It feeds the surge staffing requirements due April 30 and the alternate staffing solutions due September 30, 2027. [CC17][CC18][CC16][CC1]

  5. Bring operating room and acuity analytics to the May dashboard. The 20 percent targets need baselines by hospital, and the quarterly reporting starts June 30. [CC1][CC2]

  6. Map your workforce data offer to ARMOR and the workload capture task. Timekeeping, credentialing, and proficiency modeling land between March and September 2027, and outside workload capture is due January 30. [CC1][CC19]

  7. Keep retiree volume out of savings math. Sell it as currency and capacity, the way the October 2 brief laid out. [CC9]
