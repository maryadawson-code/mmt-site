2. Where decision authority sits after September 30

Confidence: Medium. The statute and the seat posting are primary. The internal arrangement is my reconstruction from the April 20 reorganization and later releases.

The Component Acquisition Executive owns milestone and portfolio decisions for DHA acquisition and delegates through three portfolio acquisition executives to program and product managers. The enterprise CSO confirms the three portfolio names and states that any PAE or program office can use it. [CC6] A Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Medical Digital Solutions was posted as an SES vacancy this year, and the first competitive area of interest under the CSO issued under that PAE's banner, which is the clearest public evidence the structure is operating. [CC7]

Two constraints bind the seat from outside the agency and both matter to a bid.

Commercial-first memorandum. Sec. 1822 of the FY2026 NDAA requires the contracting officer and the portfolio acquisition executive to submit a written memorandum before using non-commercial solicitation procedures. [CC5][CC13] The burden of justification now sits on the government when it wants to buy government-unique. A vendor that can show commercial customers, commercial pricing history, and a commercial roadmap is procedurally easier to buy than one that needs a PAE's signature on a justification. The enterprise CSO's own language says DHA will prioritize commercially available solutions over government-unique development. [CC6]

The contracting activity. Every award instrument runs through the DHA Contracting Activity, whose director has obligated more than $20 billion a year across the agency's portfolio. [CC14] Nothing the new executive wants to change about how DHA buys reaches an award without DHACA executing it, which makes the head of contracting activity the single most consequential working relationship in the building and the one to watch for procedural signals such as J&A cadence and CSO area-of-interest tempo.

PEO DHMS. As I reported in May, the reported arrangement is a hybrid: PEO DHMS retains executive decision authority over DHMSM, JOMIS, and EIDS while its reporting moves under the assistant director. [CC15] That rests on my own analysis and a fact sheet, with no Department memo located, so treat it as reported. If it holds, the largest programs in the account report to the new executive without being decided by him, which means the GENESIS teardown is a program he oversees and PEO DHMS executes.

Requirements. Requirements generation moved into a named J-9 function under operations and training in the April reorganization, and JCIDS was disestablished department-wide by the November 7, 2025 memo. [CC15] For a capture team the practical consequence is that operational need statements now originate in an office that sits beside acquisition, outside the buying chain, and that is the office to be in front of for anything that will be a requirement eighteen months out.

3. The vehicle, gate by gate

Confidence: High on the solicitation records. Medium on the AWACS page limits, which come from an aggregator's read of the solicitation package.

HT003826SC005, the enterprise CSO. A standing vehicle that will carry many requirements over its life. Phase structure: a brief white paper or pitch, an optional demonstration, then an invitation to selected offerors for a full proposal. Multiple awards possible per area of interest. The CSO preserves DHA's ability to award sole-source follow-on production agreements for successfully completed prototype efforts where statute allows. [CC6]

That last clause is the single most valuable structural feature of the vehicle. It converts a modest prototype agreement into a potential production position without a further competition, and the incoming executive has run that exact sequence. Kaléo's ROCS prototype went through the Medical CBRN Defense Consortium, the FDA approved the product on February 28, 2022, and the production agreement for up to 788,000 auto-injectors was executed as the follow-on to the prototype effort. [CC16][CC17] Price a prototype award under this CSO as an option on a production line.

HT003826SC005-AOI0001, AWACS. The first competitive area of interest, issued under PAE Medical Digital Solutions for end-user services across the PEO DHMS portfolio. The June industry day described a firm-fixed-price, outcome-based, centralized shared-services model on ServiceNow supporting roughly 125,000 to 150,000 active clinical and operational users and 9.5 million beneficiaries across eight service pillars and eleven user domains, replacing legacy labor-intensive support contracts, with a three-day clinical onboarding pipeline as a stated target. [CC8] The presolicitation posted August 30, 2026, and subcontracting sub-scopes carved from it carry mid-September response dates. [CC7] Aggregator reads of the package describe a Phase 1 white paper capped at ten pages plus a two-page continuity crosswalk, on roughly a ten-day response window. [CC18]

The continuity crosswalk requirement is itself a signal. DHA is buying transition risk reduction as much as capability, and a team that cannot document how it inherits an operating environment without a service gap will not clear Phase 1 regardless of solution quality.

HT003826SC003, ESS Next. Released August 5, 2026 for PEO DHMS enterprise support and software services; Phase 1 closed August 20. Awards will be fixed-price and tied to results, with DHA stating it cannot award on a level-of-effort or labor-hour basis. Phase 2 requires the contractor to draft the performance work statement and price it. Evaluation factors as reported: advancement, with solutions that do not represent a meaningful improvement ineligible for award; efficacy and outcomes linked to the proposed pricing structure; technical approach and adaptability; and price at Phase 2 only. Multiple awards anticipated. [CC9]

Read those four factors as the template for every area of interest that follows. The first factor screens on whether the offer changes anything. The second ties outcomes to price. Price on its own does not appear until Phase 2. That ordering is what a product-development executive means when he says effects come first.

Three reads for a pipeline.

First, the compression is real. Between the executive's first day and the end of his first year, the services IDIQ cliff, the EHR restructure, the research vehicle option, and two logistics transitions all land. He does not get a quiet quarter to learn the account, and the community should not expect one.

Second, the Workforce 3.0 successor is the decision to watch above the others. Six awardees, a $1.425 billion vehicle ceiling, BCG Federal at roughly $794 million obligated against a $955 million contract ceiling, a one-bidder $180.5 million core task order in 2025, and a July 2026 justification for another logical follow-on. [CC11] Whatever replaces it will be the first large services strategy written under a product-development executive, and its shape will tell you whether the outcome-priced, consolidated-vehicle direction of AWACS and ESS Next is the house style or a PEO DHMS preference.

Third, the OMNIBUS IV on-ramp lands the same week he does. Fifty-six original awardees, more than $600 million across 51 task orders, 94 percent of them issued in FY2024 through FY2026, 33 to small businesses, eight set aside for 8(a) and nine awarded to 8(a) participants. [CC12] It is the working small business channel in the portfolio, and it sits in the research directorate he now oversees.

Two cautions. No public statement by Clark on artificial intelligence policy or AI acquisition was located, so a paper that pitches to an assumed AI doctrine is guessing; pitch ambient documentation or decision support on its documented DHA justification of clinician burden and human-in-the-loop governance. And no document was located in which he uses the term Commercial Solutions Opening. His fluency with flexible instruments, middle-tier authority, and consortium agreements is documented. His enthusiasm for CSOs specifically is an inference, and it should not appear in a capture brief as his statement.

6. The products tilt and where the money is

Confidence: High on the budget figures, read from the FY2027 justification books. Medium on the tilt itself, which is inference.

The dollars and the attention are in different places this year, and that gap is the opportunity.

The digital portfolio holds the money. Information Management rises $328.4 million to $2.600 billion in the FY2027 request, the largest increase in the operations account, and it is where AWACS, ESS Next, and the GSP task orders flow. [CC30] COMP procurement is $366.728 million. [CC30] Purchased care is $22.175 billion and closed to anyone without a prime relationship. [CC30]

The growth is in operational medicine and products. Three service field-medical procurement lines total $376.3 million in FY2027 against $97.9 million two years earlier, which is more than the entire COMP procurement account. [CC30] The House FY2027 mark would give the DHA Director authority to use COMP and Army medical readiness funds for medical countermeasure procurement, pre-positioning, forward deployment, logistics, storage, and sustainment (proposed, not law). [CC31] DLA's ARMOR wargame in July 2026 stress-tested the medical supply chain with more than 120 federal and industry partners and surfaced foreign dependencies and production limits. [CC32]

Now put the executive's record on top of that. Every documented success in it is FDA-regulated materiel: ROCS, the atropine auto-injector, midazolam, VAMP, the armored ambulance. [CC16][CC24][CC25][CC33] His co-authored doctrine paper re-justifies medical logistics as a war-reserve and industrial-base problem. [CC26] My read is that his personal attention concentrates on Medical Products and medical logistics while Medical Digital Solutions keeps the dollar volume and PEO DHMS keeps the decisions on the largest programs. Firms with a products or logistics story therefore have an unusually favorable attention-to-dollar ratio in FY2027, and firms with a digital story are competing in a portfolio the executive oversees at a distance.

The likely FY2027 posture, stated as inference: regulatory-pathway literacy becomes a discriminator in a portfolio where recent competition rewarded software delivery method; industrial-base and supply-chain requirements migrate from white papers into evaluation factors and CDRLs; and the reported valley of death between funded medical prototypes and programs of record becomes a named target, since it is the exact problem his middle-tier, knowledge-point method was built to attack.

7. Teaming, conflicts, and the mid-tier squeeze

Confidence: Medium. Aggregator and trade sourced; verify each award status before acting.

Three exposures are newly acute and two of them have already cost bidders awards this year.

Advisory-versus-competitor. Swingtide holds a sole-source market intelligence, acquisition advisory, and source-selection support role on the GENESIS restructure, reported extended fourteen months in August 2026, and AIX Tech holds a $225 million DHA contracting and acquisition support IDIQ with ordering through June 2031. [CC34] Map the organizational conflict of interest boundary before a Phase 1 submission, because a discovery at Phase 2 is a withdrawal.

OEM-versus-integrator. The GENESIS teardown creates five direct platform relationships plus up to five competed services procurements, and DHA has said its reasons include eliminating pass-through costs and duplicative layers of management. [CC10] A services bidder teamed with one of the five OEMs gains platform access and inherits the appearance of privileged knowledge; a services bidder teamed with none may lack the depth to bid. Leidos loses the integrator role, keeps bridge work through the transition, and retains a seat on the $300 million Deployment Solutions IDIQ awarded this summer to twelve holders. [CC10][CC35] There is no standing answer; make the teaming call against a specific area of interest.

Size status as award risk. The DMLSS fourth-generation award to Aktarius was terminated one day after award in January 2026 when DHA determined the firm was no longer small under the NAICS code. Eagle Integrated Services graduates from 8(a) in September 2026 and is no longer small under NAICS 541611, which forces the Enterprise Assurance recompete. IRMAC Pacific's closing date was suspended by a NAICS appeal at SBA's Office of Hearings and Appeals. [CC21][CC22][CC36] Set-asides remain central to the portfolio, with a $2.4 billion total small business GSP vehicle, an 8(a) IRMAC, an 8(a) OMNIBUS IV on-ramp, and an SDVOSB DES 2, and status now disrupts awards after they are made. Re-certify your size position against every NAICS you intend to bid before the on-ramp RFP posts.

The squeeze. Services consolidate upward into fewer outcome-priced vehicles (RC3, AWACS, ESS Next) while software and product work disaggregates to direct OEM relationships (GENESIS). Both movements press on the mid-tier integrator and staff-augmentation position that historically sat between the OEM and the enterprise prime. A firm whose value proposition is integration labor is being displaced from above by consolidators and from below by direct OEM contracts. The product and prototype lanes are opening while the body-shop lanes close, and a firm in the middle should decide this fiscal year which of the two it is becoming.

8. The counterargument, stated fairly

Confidence: Medium. Analysis, not a document finding.

Four reasons a products-and-prototypes capture thesis could underperform in his first year.

The money argues against the tilt. Information Management carries $2.6 billion; COMP procurement carries $367 million. [CC30] An executive can prefer a portfolio without moving its budget, and a services firm positioned on AWACS or ESS Next will see more obligated dollars in FY2027 than a products firm positioned on a medical countermeasure.

The largest programs are decided elsewhere. If the reported PEO DHMS arrangement holds, DHMSM, JOMIS, and EIDS keep their own decision authority. [CC15] The GENESIS restructure was approved in April 2026 and its transitions were scheduled before his name went on the org chart. He inherits its outcome without having shaped its design.

Appropriations can freeze everything. The current continuing resolution runs to December 11, 2026, and a full-year CR would leave the new Private Sector Care Program account without a prior-year appropriation to continue, would not fund the $3.139 billion mandatory facilities request at all, and would repeat the FY2025 CDMRP outcome of roughly $650 million. [CC19] An acquisition executive cannot accelerate what Congress has not funded.

Execution capacity, not authority, is the binding constraint. GAO documented a projected shortfall exceeding 8,000 clinical military medical personnel, with shortfalls anticipated until at least 2027, and DHA reporting it could not hire civilians to fill the gap. [CC37] New buying authority does not staff a hospital, and the program offices that would run new prototypes are drawing from the same workforce.

The counter to the counter is the protest record. Every 2026 DHA protest located was dismissed within roughly two to five weeks of filing and none reached a merits sustain, against a governmentwide FY2025 sustain rate of 14 percent across 380 merits decisions. [CC38] The protest channel is not slowing DHA awards. For a bidder, that argues for putting the money into pre-award engagement, teaming, and the Phase 1 artifact, and little into post-award litigation, which is exactly the posture a phase-gated CSO rewards.

9. What to do in the next ninety days

Form the AWACS team this week or skip it. A ten-page white paper with a two-page continuity crosswalk on a ten-day window cannot be assembled from scratch. If the team is not already under agreement, put the effort into the second area of interest and watch this one for the evaluation pattern.

Re-certify size before the on-ramp posts. The OMNIBUS IV 8(a) RFP is expected in late September. Three DHA awards or competitions this year were disrupted by size status after the fact. Confirm your position against the on-ramp NAICS and against every NAICS on your FY2027 target list, in writing, before you spend on proposals.

Rewrite the Phase 1 template against the rubric in Section 5. Effect and measure in paragraph one; outcomes and pricing in one table; your own knowledge points and stop condition; supply chain in the first three pages if you sell products; one stated failure. This is a template change, not a pursuit-by-pursuit change, and it applies to every area of interest under HT003826SC005 for as long as the CSO stands.

Price prototype awards as production options. The CSO preserves sole-source follow-on production for successful prototypes. Build the production-phase pricing and the transition plan into the Phase 1 paper even where the area of interest does not ask for it, because the vehicle rewards offers that already know what production looks like.

Map OCI before teaming on anything GENESIS-adjacent. Swingtide's advisory role and AIX Tech's acquisition support IDIQ both sit inside the restructure. Decide which OEM, if any, you team with against a specific competed services procurement, and document the boundary before Phase 1.

Put a marker on the Workforce 3.0 successor. No solicitation exists. The ordering period closes in January. Whatever posts will be the first large services strategy written under the new executive, and the shape of it, single or multiple award, outcome-priced or labor-based, tells you whether AWACS and ESS Next are the house style. Ask the contracting officer during market research what the successor strategy is, in writing, this month.

Get in front of J-9 for anything eighteen months out. Requirements now originate in a named function that sits beside acquisition. A capability that is not a requirement yet gets shaped there, and the new executive's record says he reads requirements as effects with measures attached.

Watch the thread, then the org chart. He said in public that when positions and needs open, the community should be ready to deliver. The first named hires into DHA research, development, and acquisition from JPEO-CBRND and Army medical research tell you which portfolio gets his attention first. That is the leading indicator, and it will show up on LinkedIn before it shows up anywhere else.
