Org Intelligence · July 14, 2026

Where your legacy SDD work reports now

The April reorg and the June AD-RDA All Hands did something people keep getting wrong: they did not move the Solution Delivery Division into one box. They cut it in two. If you are still calling the old PEO Medical Systems / CIO line for a health-IT pursuit, you are calling a seat that no longer owns the decision. Here is where each piece landed, and who signs now.

Sources: DHA Org Chart (20 APR 26) and AD-RDA All Hands (9 JUN 26). Leadership moves confirmed by client, July 2026. · DHA org chart · DHA profile

The one-line answer

SDD is a functional split, not a single-box move. Its acquisition and product-delivery work follows PEO Medical Systems into AD-RDA / CAE, where the Portfolio Acquisition Executives roll up to the Component Acquisition Executive. Its infrastructure, data-center, cyber, and CIO-governance functions realign under AD-SPFO / CIO (J-6). Two lanes. Two owners. Two call paths.

The two lanes now have named owners

Acquisition / Product Delivery
James Perkins

Now officially a Portfolio Acquisition Executive (PAE) under the CAE. This confirms the AD-RDA / CAE PAE construct is stood up with named leadership. The legacy SDD and PEO MS product-delivery portfolio reports up through the PAE line to the CAE. Perkins is a PAE reporting to the CAE, not the CAE himself.

Enterprise IT Control-Plane
Pat Flanders

Now CIO only, no longer a PEO. He keeps the Chief Information Office (J-6) hat under AD-SPFO. The acquisition authority he used to carry has moved to the PAE line under AD-RDA / CAE. The old dual-hat is gone.

Why this matters for capture: the personnel split removes the old dual-hat ambiguity. Acquisition and product-delivery pursuits anchor to the PAE line under the CAE. Enterprise infrastructure, cyber, and IT-governance conversations anchor to Flanders on the CIO / J-6 side. Know which one you are in before the meeting starts.

WHAT CHANGED

The 20 APR chart broke apart the model SDD used to live inside.

Before the reorg, SDD sat inside the PEO Medical Systems / CIO (J-6) operating model, described publicly as a division of the Program Executive Office for Medical Systems. The new chart separates the J-6 control-plane and operations home from the acquisition and product-delivery home. All acquisition functions and support stay in AD-RDA, and PEO MS acquisition functions move into the new Portfolio Acquisition Executive structure. The 9 JUN All Hands then names the landing zone: PAE Medical Digital Solutions (PAE MDS) is the explicit home for the PEO DHMS and PEO MS digital portfolio.

ADJACENT, NOT THE HOME

OWHA is a transformation node, not the SDD landing zone.

The chart keeps Director, DHA at the top with the Deputy Director beneath, creates an Office of Warfighter Health Advantage (OWHA) under the Deputy Director, and retains the nine Defense Health Networks. OWHA consolidates DIG, CDAO, BID, the Digital Health Integration Office, and most SIG functions. It is a strategy-and-innovation node. It is not the home for SDD's legacy acquisition PMOs. Route CDAO and Digital Health Integration strategy conversations here, and nothing else.

WHY PAE MDS IS THE KEY LANDING ZONE

The June All Hands is the newest source, and it shows the transition below the boxes.

It states that PAE MDS ran an initial coordination meeting among the CAE, PEO DHMS, and PEO MS (SDD), launched an MDS working group, captured roughly 80 percent of the PEO DHMS and PEO MS portfolio, and drafted the PAE MDS charter, portfolio map, org-structure alignment, governance processes, and a unified IT-systems migration plan.

PEO MS transition activities include moving personnel and resources to AD-RDA / CAE, realigning supervisors to report directly to AD-RDA / CAE, aligning funding, and building a transfer plan for the ICS and MST PMOs. The market-facing read: SDD becomes part of the AD-RDA acquisition and product-management world, while its infrastructure, architecture, and cyber functions stay under CIO / J-6 in AD-SPFO whenever they are enterprise operations rather than PAE-managed delivery.

What is still open

  • Neither the 20 APR chart nor the June All Hands shows named boxes for IOD / I&O, ESA-BAD, or DCOPS. These are functional mappings, not confirmed post-reorg subdivision names.
  • The June All Hands is a transition brief, not a final manpower document. It targets full operating capability on 19 JUL 2026, with PAE portfolio and org-alignment milestones running into August and September 2026.
  • The expansion "Defense Cyber Operations" for DCOPS is not verified in public DHA sources. The evidence supports "Data Center Operations" as a J-6 service line, with a separate Cyber Security Division / RMF function.
  • Final disposition of the individual SDD PMOs (CBIS, Clinical Support, HSS, LEAP, MEDLOG IT, WMT, ICS, MST) should be validated against the final PAE MDS / MS / MP workforce map once it is issued.
  • Confirm which specific PAE portfolio Perkins leads. He is a PAE reporting to the CAE within AD-RDA / CAE, not the CAE itself and not above the PAE structure.

The analyst takeaway

For capture and market intelligence, treat legacy SDD opportunities as moving out of a PEO MS / CIO (J-6) label and into an AD-RDA / CAE PAE construct, especially PAE Medical Digital Solutions. Keep a separate call path open into AD-SPFO / CIO (J-6) for infrastructure, cyber, data-center, architecture, service-management, and enterprise IT governance.

The most defensible shorthand: SDD product delivery goes to AD-RDA / CAE → CAE → PAE (James Perkins) / PAE MDS. IOD / I&O and data-center / cyber ops go to AD-SPFO / CIO (J-6, Pat Flanders, CIO only). CDAO and Digital Health Integration strategy go to the Deputy Director / OWHA. See the full picture on the DHA org chart.
