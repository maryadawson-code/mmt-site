About the Show

Fed UP exists because federal health IT deserves honest conversation. The policies, procurement decisions, and technology bets being made right now will shape care delivery for service members and veterans for years to come.

Each episode pulls back the curtain on the real dynamics — what's driving change, what's blocking it, and what the people closest to the work actually think. No talking points. No vendor pitches. Just two practitioners working through what matters.
