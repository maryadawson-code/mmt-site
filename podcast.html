About the Show

Fed UP is a conversation between two practitioners who have spent their careers in the federal health IT ecosystem. Each episode digs into the policy decisions, budget realities, and technology bets that affect how military medicine actually works.

One filter drives every episode: does this save lives or enhance readiness? If it does, we explain why and what it means. If it doesn't, we say that too.
