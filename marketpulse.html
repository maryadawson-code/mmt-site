Common questions

Before you request a brief

What kinds of questions work best?+
Specific ones. "Who are the top 5 vendors positioned for the DHA pharmacy recompete?" will get you a better brief than "Tell me about federal health IT." The more context you give -- agency, program, time frame, competitors -- the more useful the output. We cover federal health IT broadly: VA, DHA, HHS, CMS, CDC, and the agencies that orbit them.
How fast is delivery?+
Within 24 hours. Most briefs arrive faster than that. You will get a source-cited PDF in your inbox with a summary, findings, implications, and next steps.
Will you tell me if my question is too broad?+
Yes. If your question would require a 50-page report to answer properly, we will follow up with a suggestion for how to narrow it into something useful. We would rather give you a focused 5-page brief that changes a decision than a 30-page report that sits in a folder.
Do you use primary sources?+
Always, when they exist. SAM.gov contract data, FPDS award records, USASpending obligations, Federal Register notices, agency budget justifications, and congressional testimony. Every claim in your brief links back to where we found it.
What happens after the first free report?+
Additional reports are $50 each, paid after you see the results and decide the service is worth it. No subscription, no commitment. If you need ongoing coverage or multiple reports per month, reach out and we can discuss a package.
