Common questions

Before you request a brief

What kinds of questions work best?+
Specific ones. "Who are the top 5 vendors positioned for the DHA pharmacy recompete?" will get you a better brief than "Tell me about federal health IT." The more context you give -- agency, program, time frame, competitors -- the more useful the output. We cover federal health IT broadly: VA, DHA, HHS, CMS, CDC, and the agencies that orbit them.
How fast is delivery?+
Within 24 hours. Most briefs arrive faster than that.
Will you tell me if my question is too broad?+
Yes. If your question would require a 50-page brief to answer properly, we will follow up with a suggestion for how to narrow it into something useful. We would rather give you a focused 5-page brief that changes a decision than a 30-page doorstop that sits in a folder.
Do you use primary sources?+
Always, when they exist. Every claim in your brief links back to where we found it, so your team can verify independently.
What happens after the first free brief?+
You get 1 free brief per email address. Additional briefs are $50 each. If you need ongoing coverage or multiple briefs per month, email mary@missionmeetstech.com and we can work out a package.
