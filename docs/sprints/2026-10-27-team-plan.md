# 2026-10-27 — Team plan (FHAS, four seats)

Trish Hunter (FHAS) asked for an enterprise license for a four-person BD
team. Mary's offer: four seats, Premium plus AI agent access on every seat,
$1,200 a year. Mary's instructions: build it so every future team gets the
same thing (nothing custom), set Stripe up, gate seat activation on the
invoice being paid, and credit Trish only for the unused part of the $199
Founding year she already paid.

Shipped: `docs/team-plan-spec.md` is the full design. In short: one Stripe
product "MMT Team" at $300 a seat a year (`team-pricing.json`, the $1,200 offer
stated per seat), one subscription per company with the seat emails in its
metadata, seat rows in `mp_users` keyed by the team's subscription id, seats
live only after Stripe reports a paid invoice, webhook plus hourly backstop,
a `/premium/team/` page for the billing contact, and `scripts/team-invoice.js`
to start a team and print the invoice link.

Trish's credit: $199 paid 2026-05-19 for 2026-05-19 to 2027-05-19; 204 of 365
days unused on 2026-10-27, so $111.22 off the first invoice. Her Founding
subscription is canceled by the webhook when the team invoice is paid.

Also fixed in passing: `customer.subscription.deleted` and the admin reconcile
no longer downgrade a row that is live on a different subscription.
