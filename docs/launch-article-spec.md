## Premium deliverables (every single one must be live + accurate)

1. **Personalized daily digest email at 6:30 AM ET** — assembled from
   subscriber preferences (new solicitations, contract intel, protest alerts,
   small business awards on Mondays). "If nothing new matches your filters,
   no email arrives. Only the signal."
2. **Friday Brief — 6 AM ET Fridays** — this week's summary, new solicitations,
   new awards, IDIQ status changes, three signals to act on before Monday.
3. **Monthly Intelligence Brief on the 1st** — 4–6 pages, executive summary,
   top five signals, procurement signals table, vehicle and IDIQ status,
   rotating agency spotlight. Formatted to print and hand to a CEO.
4. **Capture Intelligence Sheet** — monthly cadence. Every active federal
   health IT program, vehicle, and budget signal tracked. Action window on
   each one. Confidence label on every claim. Sources cited.
5. **Deep-dive solicitation breakdowns** — when a significant release drops.
   Evaluation criteria, incumbent vulnerability, teaming moves, win themes,
   action window, what not to do.
6. **Capture Corner on every article** — 2–4 specific implications appended
   to every newsletter article.
