## What is Mission Meets Tech?

Mission Meets Tech is an independent federal health IT intelligence platform built for GovCon BD teams, capture managers, proposal writers, and government program leaders. It covers DHA, VA, HHS, ONC, ARPA-H, and CMS — the agencies that drive federal health IT procurement.

The platform includes:
- **Twice-weekly analysis** — written intelligence on policy, procurement, and operational stakes
- **ProposalPulse** — AI-powered proposal scoring against federal evaluator criteria
- **MarketPulse** — custom market intelligence briefs delivered in 24 hours
- **Contract Tracker** — curated federal health IT contract intelligence
- **IDIQ Tracker** — vehicle ceilings, awardees, and re-compete windows
- **Capture Intelligence** — monthly signal sheets with action windows and confidence labels
- **Podcast (Fed UP)** — conversations about what's broken in federal health IT and what to do about it

MMT is funded entirely by readers. No sponsors. No advertisers. No vendor relationships.

## Using ProposalPulse

### What it does
ProposalPulse scores your federal proposal draft against 9 criteria that mirror how government evaluators actually read Section L/M responses.

### How to use it
1. Go to [missionmeetstech.com/proposal-pulse](https://missionmeetstech.com/proposal-pulse)
2. Upload your document (PDF, DOCX, or PPTX — max 25MB)
3. Select the document type (proposal, white paper, capability statement, etc.)
4. Enter your email address
5. Click "Score this document"
6. Your scorecard arrives by email in 30-90 seconds

### What the scorecard includes
- Overall score and verdict (GO / CONDITIONAL / NO-GO)
- 9 individual criterion scores:
  1. Problem Clarity
  2. Agency Alignment
  3. Technical Approach Clarity
  4. Vehicle & Acquisition Fit
  5. Team and Credibility
  6. Traction
  7. Cost / Price Credibility
  8. Competitive Position
  9. Funding Ask & Scope
- Top fix recommendation for each criterion
- Red flags that evaluators will catch
- Gold Team Review (deeper rewrite of weak sections, delivered by email)

### Pricing
- First assessment: **free**
- Additional assessments: **$19.99 each** (or **$14.99** for Premium members)
- Payment via Stripe

### Privacy
- Your document is never stored after processing
- Extracted text may be retained up to 90 days for score retrieval
- Not used to train AI models
- Do not upload classified, ITAR/EAR, or restricted material

## Using MarketPulse

### What it does
MarketPulse turns a federal health IT market question into a source-cited intelligence brief delivered within 24 hours.

### How to use it

- Not used to train AI models

## FAQ

**Is the newsletter free?**
Yes. The twice-weekly analysis, full article archive (articles older than 90 days), and podcast are free. Premium adds the capture depth layer.

**What is a Founding Member?**
$199/year, locked permanently. The first 100 subscribers get this rate for life. When 100 memberships are claimed, this rate closes and the standard annual rate ($249/year) applies.

**What is a Capture Intelligence sheet?**
A monthly intelligence brief covering specific programs, vehicles, and budget signals with active opportunity in federal health IT. Each signal includes an action window and a confidence label. Free readers see 3 preview signals. Premium subscribers see all signals with action windows.

**How does Ask MMT work?**
Premium subscribers can submit up to 2 questions per month through the Ask MMT portal. Questions are answered editorially and the best answers are published (anonymized by default) so every subscriber benefits.

**Can I switch from monthly to annual?**
Yes. Email support@missionmeetstech.com and we'll upgrade your plan and credit the difference.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Documents are not stored. Not used to train models. Encrypted in transit and at rest. See our [Security](https://missionmeetstech.com/security) and [Privacy](https://missionmeetstech.com/privacy) pages.

**Who writes the analysis?**
Mary Womack. Career federal health IT professional with experience inside DHA, VA, and civilian agencies. Independent. No vendor relationships.
