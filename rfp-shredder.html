What you get when it ships

Section L/M traceability

Every Section L instruction mapped to the Section M evaluation factor it serves. Missing-mapping flags surface before pink team.

Requirements matrix

Every "shall," "will," and "must" extracted, deduped, and tagged by category (technical, management, past performance, pricing).

Evaluator stress test

Reads each section as the SSEB would. Flags ambiguous language, missing discriminators, and Section M criteria the proposal doesn't address head-on.

Compliance gap report

Cross-references CHPL, FedRAMP, CMMC, SCA wage floors, and Section 508 against your draft. Same engine as Compliance Check.
