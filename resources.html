Federal Health IT Reference Desk

Key programs, budget sources, policy references, and acquisition tools — curated for people who work in or around defense healthcare technology.

Key Organizations

Defense Health Agency (DHA)

The combat support agency responsible for MHS operations, TRICARE, and health IT modernization across the Military Health System.

Veterans Affairs (VA) OIT

The Office of Information and Technology manages VA's health IT portfolio including EHR modernization and interoperability programs.

Federal Electronic Health Record Modernization (FEHRM)

The joint program office responsible for deploying a single, shared EHR across DoD and VA.

DoD CIO / DISA

Oversees defense IT policy, cybersecurity standards, and the infrastructure backbone that health systems depend on.

Budget & Policy Sources

DoD Comptroller Budget Documents

Annual defense health program budget justification materials. The primary source for understanding where health IT dollars are going.

Congressional Research Service (CRS)

Non-partisan policy analysis on defense health, VA healthcare, and military personnel programs. High-quality synthesis of legislative action.

Government Accountability Office (GAO)

Audit and evaluation reports covering IT modernization, cost overruns, program effectiveness, and acquisition oversight.

National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA)

The annual defense policy bill that sets direction for military health programs, technology mandates, and acquisition reform.

Acquisition & Contracting

SAM.gov

The System for Award Management — where federal opportunities are posted and contract data is published.

FPDS.gov

Federal Procurement Data System — the authoritative source for historical contract award data across all federal agencies.

USAspending.gov

Federal spending transparency — track agency spending, contract awards, and budget execution across health programs.

OSDBU Resources

Small business contracting guidance from the Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization at DoD and VA.
