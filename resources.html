Acquisition & Contracting

FAR, DFARS, OTAs, 8(a), SBIR — the acquisition landscape is the battlefield for every defense health vendor.

FAR Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR)

The Bible. Know it. Everything flows from here. Use the search function — it's better than you think.

DFARS Defense FAR Supplement (DFARS)

DoD-specific acquisition rules layered on top of FAR. Cybersecurity, data rights, and health IT security requirements live here.

SBA SBA 8(a) Business Development

Eligibility, application, and current rules. Essential reading after Hegseth's January 2026 enforcement sweep.

SBIR SBIR/STTR Program Portal

DoD SBIR/STTR solicitations, award data, and program status. Track the lapse impact here.

OTA DoD OTA Guide (2023)

Other Transaction Authority guidance. The fast lane for defense health tech procurement — when program offices know how to use it.

SAM.gov SAM.gov Contract Opportunities

Federal contract solicitations. Set filters to DHA/VA with NAICS codes for health IT to surface relevant opportunities fast.
