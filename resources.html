Resources

Federal Health IT Resource Guide

Curated links to the agencies, programs, budget documents, and data sources that shape federal health IT. Bookmarks for practitioners.

Key Agencies & Programs

Defense Health Agency (DHA)

The unified medical authority for the Military Health System. Oversees healthcare delivery, IT modernization, and MHS GENESIS deployment across DoD.

health.mil

VA Office of Information & Technology

Leads IT strategy and infrastructure for the Department of Veterans Affairs, including EHR modernization and digital services.

oit.va.gov

Federal Electronic Health Record Modernization (FEHRM)

The interagency program office driving a single, shared EHR across DoD and VA for seamless care transitions.

fehrm.gov

DARPA Biological Technologies Office

Advanced research programs at the intersection of biology, health, and technology for national security applications.

darpa.mil/bto

DoD Comptroller

Official source for Defense budget documents, justification materials, and financial management policy.

comptroller.defense.gov

GAO Health Care Reports

Government Accountability Office audits, evaluations, and reports on federal healthcare programs and IT systems.

gao.gov/health-care

Acquisition & Contracting

SAM.gov

System for Award Management — the official U.S. government system for entity registration, contract opportunities, and award data.

sam.gov

USASpending.gov

Track federal spending by agency, contractor, program, and geography. Essential for competitive intelligence.

usaspending.gov

Congressional Research Service

Nonpartisan policy research for Congress. Excellent source for defense health, VA, and appropriations analysis.

crsreports.congress.gov
