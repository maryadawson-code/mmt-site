April 2026: The DHA Reorg Quarter

A retrospective. What shipped on the procurement side, what shipped in MMT coverage, and what to track in May.

Cadence note (effective May 2026). Going forward the monthly cadence is absorbed by Capture Intelligence Sheets. The May 2026 Capture Intelligence Sheet (VA Enterprise Imaging) is published here. This April retrospective is the bridge issue.

Bottom line up front

April was the DHA reorganization quarter. The agency issued a new structure on April 20 with three Portfolio Acquisition Executives, two Acting senior seats, and a Full Operational Capability target of July 19. Nine days later the AD-RDA Town Hall named the leadership: RDML Ivonne Arena (Acting AD-RDA / CAE), BG Bill A. Soliz (Acting AD-HCAO), and RDML Tracy Farrill (Director, Office of Warfighter Health Advantage, just appointed). Eighty days from the Town Hall to FOC.

Underneath the org chart, three procurements moved: VA EHRM restarted with four Michigan sites going live April 11 (first wave of 13 planned 2026 deployments), PEO DHMS Deployment Solutions HT003826RE001 closed proposals on April 21 ($300M ceiling, one base year plus four options), and the T4NG2 protest cleared with the final awardee count reported at 33 (up from 30 originally posted on SAM).

The DHA reorganization in four acts

Act 1 (April 20). New structure issues.

The Defense Health Agency reorganized under NDAA Section 1802 (Public Law 119-60, signed December 18, 2025) and the Hegseth "Warfighting Acquisition System" memorandum of November 7, 2025. Sixteen named changes. Five of them move pipelines: the Portfolio Acquisition Executive transition, the Office of Warfighter Health Advantage consolidation, CIO / J-6 leaving acquisition, AD-SPFO becoming the statutory Chief Financial Officer, and the new J-9 Requirements function. The full mapping is in the April 29 Capture Corner.

Act 2 (April 21). IOC structure operational.

Day 2 / IOC org chart names the four Acting program execs underneath AD-RDA: Mr. James Perkins (Acting PEO DHMS, with three DHMS PMOs), COL Marcus Moss (Acting PEO Medical Systems, six PMOs that absorb into PAE Medical Software & Business Systems by FOC), Mrs. Madeleine Friedman (Acting PAE Medical Services, five PMOs), and Ms. Caitlyn Felkoski (Acting PAE Medical Products NH-IV, five PMOs). DHACA (Mr. James Watkins) and DHA R&D (MG Paula Lodi) continue as direct reports. The full IOC structure is mapped on the DHA org chart.

Act 3 (April 29). AD-RDA Town Hall.

The Town Hall named the senior trio. Two seats Acting, one newly seated. The original April 29 issue called OWHA "empty" pending appointment; the corrected and reissued version names RDML Tracy Farrill at OWHA, RDML Ivonne Arena at AD-RDA Acting, and BG Bill A. Soliz at AD-HCAO Acting. The Town Hall slides also surfaced a governance change that did not make the org chart: the IT Integration Board and the Acquisition Integration Board are gone. Their panels (IM/IT Panel, RD&A Panel) survive and feed the Warfighter Modernization and Support Integration Board directly. The internal explanation: prevent "shopping for yes."

Act 4 (May to July). FOC stand-up.

By July 19, three PAEs stand up fully (Medical Software & Business Systems, Medical Services, Medical Products), PEO DHMS reporting transitions under AD-RDA, USAHCA workload and staff transition to DHACA, and the IOC Acting designations resolve to permanent or roll forward. Anything still Acting on July 19 signals slippage on the agency's own timeline, not the NDAA backstop (December 2027).

What moved on the procurement side

VA EHRM restart

Four Michigan sites went live April 11, 2026 (Iron Mountain, Saginaw, Battle Creek, Detroit). First wave of 13 sites planned for 2026. Wave 2 (June): Chillicothe OH, Cincinnati OH, Cincinnati Fort Thomas KY, Dayton OH. Wave 3 (August): Fort Wayne IN, Marion IN, Indianapolis Roudebush IN. Wave 4 (October): Cleveland OH, Alaska VA Healthcare System. Each site generates modality refresh and integration task order demand against the Joint VA/DoD Radiology and Imaging IDIQ (36H79725R0003, $6.5B ceiling, awards expected Q3 FY2026). Detailed signal map in the May 1 Capture Intelligence Sheet.

PEO DHMS Deployment Solutions

Solicitation HT003826RE001. $300 million ceiling, one base year plus four option years, NAICS 541512. Scope: deployment, training, change management, and data migration support for DHMS products including MHS GENESIS. Proposals closed April 21. This is the contract that backfills the role DHITUC played for site-level deployment services. Tracked in the IDIQ Tracker and Contract Tracker.

T4NG2 protest resolution

The T4NG2 protest cleared in March. SAM listed 30 original awardees; the post-protest final roster is reported at 33. T4NG2 is the primary VA IT services vehicle and the access lane for VA EHRM site-by-site integration task orders. Vehicle baseline updated in data/idiq-vehicles.json: $60.7B ceiling, Active / Awarded, NAICS 541512.

The contract data corrections

Three contract-tracker corrections worth knowing about. CIO-SP4 was canceled (SAM Amendment 0017 on RFP 75N98121R00001); CIO-SP3 bridges through April 29, 2027. ITES-3H is legacy / expiring; the 2023 extension covers 16 vendors through February 19, 2026. The DHMSM / MHS GENESIS prime is Leidos Partnership for Defense Health, not Oracle; deployment completed March 2024 and the program is in sustainment / optimization with a ceiling that grew from $4.3B to over $5.5B.

The pattern, stated plainly

April's headline is structural. The DHA reorg is statutory compliance with NDAA Section 1802, but the IOC-to-FOC stand-up runs through July 19 on the agency's own timeline. The contracts that move during this window get shaped by whoever fills the Acting seats. AD-RDA Acting. HCAO Acting. OWHA newly seated. Eighty days, less the one I just used to write this brief.
