Questions

Does everything I currently receive stay free?

Yes. The twice-weekly analysis, the full article archive, and the podcast are all staying free. Premium adds a deeper layer. It does not remove anything.

What is the Founding Member rate?

$199/year, locked in permanently for the first 100 subscribers. As long as you stay subscribed, your rate never increases. When 100 memberships are claimed, this rate closes and the standard annual rate ($249/year) applies.

What is a Capture Intelligence sheet?

A monthly intelligence brief covering the specific programs, vehicles, and budget signals with active opportunity in federal health IT. Each signal includes an action window (what to do and when) and a confidence label (High / Medium / Speculative). Free readers get 3 preview signals. Premium subscribers get all signals immediately, with every action window.

Can I switch from monthly to annual?

Yes. Email mary@missionmeetstech.com and we will upgrade your plan and credit the difference.

What is your refund policy?

Annual subscribers who cancel within 14 days of their renewal date receive a full refund. Monthly subscribers can cancel anytime before their next billing date.
