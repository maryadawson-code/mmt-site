Questions

Is this ChatGPT with a logo?

No. It answers from named federal sources and the MMT archive, and it shows the sources. Ask both the same question about a vehicle and compare where the numbers came from.

Can I use it for a bid decision?

Use it for the research behind the decision. Check the sources it gives you. That is why they are there.

Does it train on my questions?

No. Same policy as ProposalPulse and MarketPulse. Your question and the answer are kept in your own browser and in MMT's usage log for quota counting; the AI provider does not train on them. Details at missionmeetstech.com/security.

How many questions do I get?

a month free. a month on Premium. pooled on Institutional. Counts reset on the 1st.

What if it does not know?

It says so. It does not guess. If the answer is not in the sources it reads, you get "I don't have a source for that" and a suggestion for where to look.

What is Analyst Q&A?

The human channel. Premium members can send Mary a question a month (two on Founding). The best answers run in the monthly Q&A issue.
