WISeR

Wasteful and Inappropriate Service Reduction model. A CMS Innovation Center model in six states in which technology vendors review prior authorization requests for selected Medicare fee-for-service services, with a clinician required before any non-affirmation. The vendor is paid 25% of averted spending, adjusted by a quality score, and loses the payment if the denial is overturned on appeal.

Why It Matters

WISeR is the test case for incentive design standing in for measurement. If a program paid on averted spending is ordered to add an outside validator, other programs may copy the structure quickly.

Contractor Note

As of mid-August 2026 CMS had released no denial, appeal or overturn rates. CMS says it audits vendors against coverage criteria and may remove one with a high inaccuracy rate, but I found no published audit method, sample size or result, and no separate validation contract. Congress has pressed on the model; a House committee amendment would block FY2027 funding.

From the Ask MMT Founding Member brief Who Proves the Decision Was Right (November 13, 2026).
