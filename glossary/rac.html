RAC

Recovery Audit Contractor. Medicare fee-for-service post-payment review contractors paid a contingency fee on the improper payments they identify. CMS program improvements set a first-level appeal overturn limit below 10% and added a separate Recovery Auditor Validation Contractor that independently checks RAC accuracy. The validation contract was awarded to J29 in February 2026 at $17.2M over five years as a total small business set-aside, per a trade compilation.

Why It Matters

If you are independent of operations, the validation line is small but durable, and one award was a total small business set-aside.

Contractor Note

The recovery audit program is the precedent for separating the validator from the operator when the operator is paid from the result. WISeR vendors are paid a share of averted spending with no separate validator; expect that structure to be questioned.

From the Ask MMT Founding Member brief Who Proves the Decision Was Right (November 13, 2026).
