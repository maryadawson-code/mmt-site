High-impact AI

OMB M-25-21 designation. Under OMB Memorandum M-25-21 (April 2025), an AI use is high-impact when its output is a principal basis for decisions on benefits, allocation of care under public insurance, continued eligibility, or fraud detection. The label triggers pre-deployment testing, an impact assessment, ongoing monitoring, human oversight and a working remedy or appeal path. Agencies apply it unevenly: in the FY2025 inventories VA reported 215 of the 445 high-impact uses governmentwide, while HHS designated 2 of its 447 reported uses.

Why It Matters

Whether a review, payment or eligibility tool carries the high-impact label decides whether the agency must test it before deployment and evaluate it on data the vendor cannot see. That is the difference between a clause and an attestation.

Contractor Note

The floor covers fewer systems than it appears to. Keeping a human on the decision can serve both as a safeguard and as a reason not to apply the stricter rules. Check the agency's AI use case inventory for the designation before assuming the M-25-21 requirements are in your contract.
