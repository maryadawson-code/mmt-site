Federal Health IT Glossary

57 terms across five pillars. Each entry has an expanded definition, context on why it matters, and links to related terms.

← Back to Resources

MHS Technology

15 terms

ASD(HA)

Assistant Secretary of Defense for Health Affairs. The top civilian health policy official in the Pentagon.

DHA

Defense Health Agency. Runs military healthcare operations, TRICARE, pharmacy, and health IT.

DHP

Defense Health Program. The $40+ billion annual budget line that funds military healthcare.

EHR

Electronic Health Record. The digital medical record system. DoD uses MHS GENESIS (Oracle Health). VA is deploying the same platform.

JOMIS

Joint Operational Medicine Information Systems. Medical IT for deployed and combat environments.

MHS

Military Health System. The entire enterprise: DHA, Service medical commands, MTFs, TRICARE, and the health IT infrastructure that connects them.

MHS GENESIS

The DoD's electronic health record, built on Oracle Health (formerly Cerner). Deployed across military hospitals and clinics.

MTF

Military Treatment Facility. A military hospital or clinic. There are roughly 700 worldwide, from Walter Reed to battalion aid stations.

NDAA

National Defense Authorization Act. Annual legislation that sets defense policy and spending priorities.

OPMED

Operational Medical Systems (formerly PEO DHMS). DoD's lead for medical development and acquisition, including MHS GENESIS and deployed medical IT.

Role 1 / 2 / 3 (Medical Echelons)

Military medical echelons that determine what care capability and EHR systems are deployed at each level.

TRICARE

The healthcare program for military members, retirees, and families. Covers 9.6M beneficiaries. Managed by DHA through regional contractors.

CRS

Congressional Research Service. Nonpartisan policy research arm of Congress.

GAO

Government Accountability Office. Congress's investigative arm.

Service Components

The military departments (Army, Navy, Air Force) and their subordinate organizations, plus Marines, Space Force, and Coast Guard.

VA Health IT

6 terms

VA EHR (Oracle Health)

The VA's electronic health record modernization program, now under Oracle Health (formerly Cerner).

VistA

Veterans Health Information Systems and Technology Architecture. VA's legacy EHR, built in-house starting in the 1970s.

VHA

Veterans Health Administration. The healthcare delivery arm of the VA. Operates the hospitals, clinics, and care networks.

JLV

Joint Longitudinal Viewer. A web-based tool that gives VA and DoD providers read-only access to a consolidated view of a patient's medical history across both systems.

T4NG

Transformation Twenty-One Total Technology Next Generation. VA's primary IT services contract vehicle. $22.3B ceiling.

Connected Care

VA's Office of Connected Care. Runs telehealth, remote patient monitoring, VA Video Connect, and the My HealtheVet patient portal.

Contracting & Acquisition

19 terms

FAR

Federal Acquisition Regulation. The primary rule book governing how the federal government buys goods and services.

IDIQ

Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity. A contract vehicle that sets terms and ceiling but lets the government order as needed via task orders.

GWAC

Government-Wide Acquisition Contract. Multi-agency contract vehicles that any federal agency can use to buy IT products and services.

BPA

Blanket Purchase Agreement. A streamlined ordering mechanism set up against an existing GSA Schedule contract.

Task Order

The mechanism by which actual work is issued under IDIQ contracts, GWACs, and BPAs.

RFP

Request for Proposal. The formal solicitation. The government saying "submit your proposal by this date."

RFI

Request for Information. The government asking industry "what can you do?" before writing a formal solicitation.

SOW

Statement of Work. The contract document that specifies exactly what the contractor must do, how, and when.

PWS

Performance Work Statement. Tells the contractor what outcomes to deliver (vs. SOW which tells them how).

CO / KO

Contracting Officer. The individual with legal authority to enter into, administer, and terminate contracts on behalf of the U.S. government.

COR

Contracting Officer's Representative. The government employee designated to oversee day-to-day contractor performance after award.

pWin

Probability of Win. A capture management metric estimating the likelihood of winning a contract.

OTA

Other Transaction Authority. A faster procurement path that bypasses traditional FAR contracting rules.

Fixed-Price vs. Cost-Plus

The two fundamental contract pricing structures in federal contracting.

Small Business Set-Asides

Federal contracting categories that restrict competition to qualifying small businesses.

SAM.gov

System for Award Management. The single government database for vendor registration and contract opportunities.

GovCon

Government Contracting. The industry of selling products and services to federal agencies.

PM / DPM

Program Manager / Deputy Program Manager. The person responsible for overall program success.

FY (Fiscal Year)

The federal fiscal year runs October 1 to September 30. FY2026 started October 1, 2025.

Compliance & Cybersecurity

8 terms

ATO

Authority to Operate. The security approval a system needs before it can go live on a government network.

FedRAMP

Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program. The government's security certification for cloud services.

CMMC

Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification. The DoD framework requiring contractors to meet specific cybersecurity standards.

NIST SP 800-171

The NIST standard for protecting Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) in non-federal systems.

NIST SP 800-53

The comprehensive catalog of security and privacy controls for federal information systems.

Zero Trust

DoD's mandated security architecture. "Never trust, always verify." Assumes breach and verifies every access request.

STIGs

Security Technical Implementation Guides. DISA's configuration standards for hardening IT systems on DoD networks.

COTS

Commercial Off-the-Shelf. Software or hardware bought as-is from a vendor rather than custom-built.

Interoperability

9 terms

FHIR

Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (pronounced "fire"). The modern data standard for exchanging health information between systems.

HL7

Health Level Seven. The foundational family of healthcare data exchange standards that predates FHIR.

USCDI

United States Core Data for Interoperability. The standardized set of health data classes that systems must be able to exchange.

TEFCA

Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement. The national infrastructure for health data exchange.

FEHRM

Federal Electronic Health Record Modernization office. Coordinates the single, common EHR across DoD, VA, Coast Guard, and NOAA.

Joint HIE

Joint Health Information Exchange. The health data exchange network connecting DoD, VA, and community partners.

eHealth Exchange

The largest health information exchange network in the U.S. Connects federal agencies, states, and health systems.

Carequality

A national interoperability framework that enables health data exchange between participating networks.

DirectTrust

A national framework for secure, directed health information exchange using Direct messaging.
