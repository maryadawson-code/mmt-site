SMRC

Supplemental Medical Review Contractor. The CMS contractor that performs medical reviews on topics CMS directs, outside the regional claims contractors' routine review. The November 1, 2026 CMS forecast lists a new Supplemental Medical Review Contract at $100 million or more, with a solicitation planned for December 15, 2026 and award in May 2027.

Why It Matters

Medical review is where software now does the reading and a clinician signs the denial. The SMRC recompete is the next place that split gets written into contract language.

Contractor Note

Watch whether the solicitation includes accuracy or evaluation terms for AI-assisted review. If it does, that is the first large CMS review buy to write the proof into the operating contract.
