HHS is two stories now, not one. The mission-unique side still buys at the operating division: NIH for research IT, CMS for claims, FDA for surveillance, ARPA-H for OTA-funded R&D. The common side (laptops, cloud, cybersecurity, advisory services) now flows through the new OMAS strategic buying centers under EO 14240. Your BD calendar needs both maps.
