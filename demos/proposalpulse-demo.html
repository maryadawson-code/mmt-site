Gold Team Review takes the scorecard further without asking you to do more.

Once the scorecard is finished, ProposalPulse automatically runs a second review that strengthens the draft and checks whether the rewrite is actually better.

Gold Team running
What happens here
ProposalPulse does not stop at scoring. It also produces a stronger version of the work.
One pass rewrites the sections. A second pass reviews the rewrite for quality, confidence, and improvement before the final Gold Team Review is emailed out.
