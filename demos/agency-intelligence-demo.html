Beat 3 · IHS Profile

Indian Health Service · PATH EHR

Federal care delivery + EHR modernization

IHS

Indian Health Service

Federal HIT modernization across IHS direct service, Tribal, and Urban Indian (I/T/U) health programs. PATH EHR is the multi-year replacement and interoperability program.

Type: HHS operating division · Updated 2026-05-13
PATH EHR Modernization

Confirmed facts

  • IHS HIT is modernizing the agency's health IT infrastructure across direct, Tribal, and Urban Indian (I/T/U) health programs.
  • PATH EHR replaces legacy systems, improves interoperability, supports continuity of care, and provides centralized infrastructure and support.
  • Model accommodates IHS direct service operations and partnerships with Tribal and Urban Indian organizations.
Source: IHS Health IT Modernization · verified 2026-05-13 high confidence
Contractor Read

IHS PATH is a multi-year EHR modernization with three customer surfaces (direct service, Tribal, Urban). Capture posture: position around interoperability with VA and DHA, centralized support and disaster recovery, and the Tribal/Urban consultation model. Per-site point solutions are the wrong shape; managed-service and platform plays fit.

CDC

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

CDC's Public Health Data Strategy defines 2026 milestones across surveillance modernization, interoperability, and STLT (state, tribal, local, territorial) partner capacity.

Type: HHS operating division · Updated 2026-05-13

WindowMilestoneWhy it matters

Contractor Read

CDC's DMI + PHDS framing is the leading indicator for both CDC procurements and downstream state/tribal/local/territorial buys. Read 2026 milestones as theme signals (eCR, mortality FHIR, NBS, 1CDP, MDN governance) and position with reusable services that scale across jurisdictions, not bespoke point solutions.
