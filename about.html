About Mission Meets Tech

The Gap Is the Story

The U.S. military can synchronize a kill chain across three continents in milliseconds. It still cannot reliably move a service member's medical record from one base to another.

That gap between what we build for the battlefield and what we fail to build for the clinic is the story I cover.

Mary Womack

Origin

Why This Exists

I started Mission Meets Tech because I kept watching the same thing happen: critical federal health IT decisions getting made without the right people having the right information at the right time. The Defense Health Program is a $40+ billion annual operation. The stakes are readiness, recovery, and lives.

I've spent my career in this space. I've seen what happens when a contract award reshuffles a program overnight, when a leadership change at DHA sends shockwaves through portfolios, when a budget line item quietly kills a modernization effort that thousands of people were counting on.

Does this save lives or enhance readiness?

The only filter that matters.

No vendor spin. No consultant fog. Just the intelligence you need to make better decisions this week.

Principles

What I Believe

Evidence over opinion.

Claims backed by dates, dollars, entities, and operational consequences. If I can't verify it, I flag it.

How I Analyze

Five frameworks shape how I evaluate every policy shift, contract signal, and technology claim.

Framework

Readiness Test

Does this increase readiness or operational capability? Every dollar, contract, and policy decision gets measured against whether it makes the force more capable and ready.

Framework

Governance Paradox

Where is bureaucracy blocking progress vs. protecting safety? Is a governance structure a legitimate safeguard or a mission-killing bottleneck?

Framework

Third Way Architecture

What realistic bridge exists between legacy constraints and modern delivery? The answer lives within existing infrastructure, authorities, and workforce realities.

Framework

Human Cost

Who pays when this fails? Policy connects to people. The stakes are always concrete: a medic waiting on a system, a veteran navigating a broken process.
