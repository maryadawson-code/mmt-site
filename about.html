About the Platform

Intelligence built for the
people at the edge.

Federal health IT is too important — and too complex — to be covered with press releases and surface-level takes. Mission Meets Tech translates policy into practice for the people who build, buy, and operate the systems that support military and veteran healthcare.

One editorial filter: Does this help someone save lives or enhance readiness?

Why This Exists

The gap isn't technical.
It's translational.

Federal health IT sits at the intersection of national security, healthcare delivery, and technology modernization. The decisions made inside DHA, VA, and Congress ripple through an ecosystem of contractors, clinicians, and service members who depend on these systems working.

Most coverage of this space is either too shallow to be useful or too dense to be actionable. Mission Meets Tech bridges that gap — delivering clear, evidence-based analysis that helps operators, executives, and policy professionals understand what's actually happening and what it means for their work.

Meet the Founder

At the intersection of strategy and real-world impact.

Mary works at the intersection of health strategy and real-world impact, spanning government, digital health, and innovation. Her career covers federal initiatives and industry partnerships, with one constant thread: advancing healthcare solutions that truly improve lives.

Her sweet spot is helping leaders navigate complexity — translating government goals into actionable roadmaps, accelerating adoption of AI and emerging tech in healthcare delivery, and building partnerships that turn ideas into working systems.

Her track record spans DoD, DHA, VA, and civilian agencies, where she has led initiatives that reduce adoption risk and accelerate delivery to the point of care. She launched Mission Meets Tech because the defense health community deserves independent, evidence-based intelligence that tells the truth — not just what sells.

What We Believe

The principles behind every piece of analysis.

01

Evidence Over Opinion

Claims backed by dates, dollars, entities, and operational consequences. No speculation dressed up as analysis.

02

Independence Matters

Mission Meets Tech is an independent platform. Our analysis is not shaped by employer affiliation or vendor relationships.

03

Clarity Is Kindness

Complex policy deserves clear explanation, not jargon walls. We translate complexity into action without oversimplifying.

04

Mission First

Everything passes through one question: does this help save lives or enhance readiness? If not, we don't cover it.
