Homepage strategy

Shift from blog homepage to intelligence briefing hub.

Your site already has authority, recurring analysis, and proprietary-looking utility. The redesign simply makes those strengths visible faster and in a more premium order.

Problem
Too many homepage jobs compete at once: subscribe, browse, validate trust, explain the founder story, and surface tools.
Fix
Use a newsroom + intelligence-center structure with one clear featured story and a stronger system for pathways and proof.
Result
Faster comprehension, stronger credibility, and better conversion into newsletter subscribers and repeat readers.
