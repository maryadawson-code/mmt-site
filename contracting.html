The vehicles, mechanics, and intelligence you need to win federal health IT work.

▶ CIO-SP3 NIH/NITAAC $40B Full & open, SB pools Active (extended) Primary IT GWAC for DHA and VA health IT task orders

CIO-SP3 — Detailed Analysis

CIO-SP3 (Chief Information Officer-Solutions and Partners 3) is the workhorse IT GWAC managed by NITAAC at the National Institutes of Health. Originally awarded in 2012 with a $40B ceiling, it has been extended multiple times due to the CIO-SP4 cancellation.

Why it matters for health IT: DHA, VA, and HHS agencies route a significant volume of health IT task orders through CIO-SP3. If you are pursuing federal health IT work and are not on this vehicle, you are likely teaming with someone who is.

Current dynamics: With CIO-SP4 cancelled, CIO-SP3 holders have an extended runway. Agencies dependent on NITAAC GWACs have no near-term alternative at this scale. Watch NITAAC for signals on a potential rebid or bridge vehicle.

NAICS codes: 541512 (primary), 541511, 541519, 518210, 611420
▶ CIO-SP4 NIH/NITAAC Was $50B Was tiered Cancelled (Jan 2026) Cancellation after years of protests reshuffled GWAC landscape

CIO-SP4 — Post-Cancellation Analysis

CIO-SP4 was supposed to be the $50B successor to CIO-SP3. After years of bid protests, legal challenges, and multiple re-evaluations, NITAAC cancelled the solicitation in January 2026.

What happened: The tiered evaluation approach drew sustained protests from firms excluded in early rounds. GAO sustained multiple protests. NITAAC attempted corrective actions but could not resolve the structural issues in the solicitation design.

Impact: Firms that invested heavily in the CIO-SP4 bid process face sunk costs with no vehicle to show for it. Agencies must redistribute task orders across CIO-SP3 (extended), OASIS+, and SEWP VI. The GWAC landscape is more fragmented as a result.

What to watch: Whether NITAAC attempts a streamlined rebid, or whether GSA and NASA SEWP absorb the demand permanently. This reshuffles competitive positioning across the federal IT market.
▶ OASIS+ GSA Unlimited Full & open, SB domains Active Professional services across all agencies, including health IT consulting

OASIS+ — Detailed Analysis

OASIS+ is GSA's next-generation professional services vehicle with an unlimited ceiling. It replaces OASIS (One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services) and covers management consulting, scientific, engineering, logistics, and IT services.

Health IT relevance: OASIS+ is increasingly used for health IT consulting, program management, and advisory services at DHA and VA. It is not an IT product vehicle, but it is where health IT strategy, PMO support, and implementation oversight work lands.

Small business: OASIS+ has dedicated small business domains. With CIO-SP4 cancelled, more health IT-adjacent work may flow through OASIS+ small business pools.

Key differentiator: Unlimited ceiling and broad scope make OASIS+ the default "catch-all" for professional services. If the work is not pure IT product/infrastructure, it likely fits here.
▶ T4NG VA TAC $22.3B SDVOSB pools Active VA's primary vehicle for IT services including health IT modernization

T4NG — Detailed Analysis

Transformation Twenty-One Total Technology Next Generation (T4NG) is VA's primary IT services IDIQ, managed by the VA Technology Acquisition Center (TAC). The $22.3B ceiling covers IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, software development, cloud, and health IT modernization.

Health IT relevance: T4NG is where VA's biggest health IT task orders land, including EHR-adjacent work, clinical systems integration, data analytics, and telehealth infrastructure. If you work VA health IT, T4NG is essential.

SDVOSB dynamics: VA has strong SDVOSB set-aside requirements. Many T4NG task orders are set aside for verified SDVOSBs or require significant SDVOSB subcontracting. Joint ventures between large primes and SDVOSBs are common.

NAICS: 541512, 541511, 541519, 518210, 541513
▶ MHS EITS DHA Multi-billion Full & open Active Enterprise IT infrastructure for the entire Military Health System

MHS EITS — Detailed Analysis

Military Health System Enterprise IT Services (MHS EITS) is DHA's enterprise IT infrastructure contract. It covers network operations, end-user support, cybersecurity, data center management, and the IT backbone that all MHS clinical and administrative systems run on.

Health IT relevance: MHS EITS is the plumbing beneath MHS GENESIS. The EHR cannot function without the network, endpoint, and security infrastructure this contract provides. Performance on EITS directly affects clinical system availability at Military Treatment Facilities.

Competitive landscape: Large defense IT primes dominate. Subcontracting opportunities exist in specialized areas like clinical device integration, cybersecurity monitoring, and network optimization at MTFs.

Key risk: EITS performance has been scrutinized when MHS GENESIS deployment issues trace back to infrastructure readiness. Expect continued oversight pressure.
▶ MQS2-NG DHA TBD TBD Pre-solicitation Medical quality systems, patient safety IT, clinical decision support

MQS2-NG — Detailed Analysis

Medical Quality Systems Support Next Generation (MQS2-NG) is the follow-on to DHA's MQS2 contract for clinical quality, patient safety IT systems, and clinical decision support. It is currently in pre-solicitation.

Health IT relevance: This vehicle covers the clinical quality layer: adverse event tracking, quality metrics dashboards, clinical decision support tools, and patient safety reporting. It sits at the intersection of IT and clinical operations.

Opportunity: Pre-solicitation status means the requirements are still being shaped. Firms with clinical quality and patient safety expertise should monitor RFI/Sources Sought notices on SAM.gov for DHA Contracting Activity (DHACA) releases.

What to watch: Set-aside decisions and whether DHA bundles this with other clinical support contracts or keeps it standalone.
▶ VHA IHT 2.0 VA TBD SDVOSB Active VA integrated health technologies services

VHA IHT 2.0 — Detailed Analysis

VHA Integrated Health Technologies (IHT) 2.0 is VA's vehicle for health technology services including clinical systems integration, telehealth platforms, mobile health, and health data interoperability.

Health IT relevance: IHT 2.0 is specifically scoped for health technology, not general IT. Task orders cover VistA integration, connected care platforms, clinical workflow tools, and health data exchange. It is closer to the patient than T4NG's infrastructure focus.

SDVOSB focus: IHT 2.0 has significant SDVOSB set-aside requirements, creating opportunities for verified small businesses with health technology expertise.

Strategic note: As VA navigates its EHR modernization challenges, IHT 2.0 task orders for integration, interoperability, and connected care are likely to increase.
▶ GSA MAS Health IT GSA Open Multiple Active GSA Schedule with Health IT Special Item Numbers

GSA MAS Health IT — Detailed Analysis

GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) includes Health IT Special Item Numbers (SINs) that cover EHR products, health data analytics, telehealth platforms, medical device integration, and clinical IT services.

Health IT relevance: GSA MAS is the entry-level vehicle for health IT products and services. Lower barriers to entry than GWACs. Commonly used for COTS health IT products, SaaS platforms, and smaller-scale implementations.

How agencies use it: DHA and VA use GSA MAS for commercial health IT products, particularly when the requirement is well-defined and competitive pricing is available through GSA Advantage or eBuy.

Key SINs: 54151S (IT Professional Services), 54151HEAL (Health IT Services), 518210C (Cloud), OLM (Order-Level Materials for IT hardware/integration)

CIO-SP4 Cancellation: What Happened

In January 2026, NITAAC cancelled the CIO-SP4 solicitation after years of bid protests and legal challenges. The $50B GWAC was intended to replace CIO-SP3, which has been extended as a result.

The cancellation forces agencies to rely on existing vehicles (CIO-SP3, OASIS+, SEWP VI) and has created uncertainty for firms that invested heavily in the CIO-SP4 bid process. Watch for a potential rebid or alternative acquisition strategy from NITAAC.

Set-Aside Types

SDVOSB

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business

Required for many VA and some DHA contracts. VA has specific SDVOSB verification through SBA.

WOSB

Women-Owned Small Business

Eligible for set-asides in underrepresented industries. Certification through SBA.

8(a) Business Development

SBA Disadvantaged Small Business Program

SBA program for disadvantaged small businesses. Sole-source authority up to $4.5M for services.

HUBZone

Historically Underutilized Business Zones

Historically Underutilized Business Zones. Price evaluation preference and sole-source authority.
