Federal Health IT Glossary

Plain-language definitions for the acronyms, frameworks, and programs that run the federal health IT market. Written for contractors, PMs, and anyone tired of guessing what the abbreviation means.

A

ADVOCATE

Agentic AI-Enabled Cardiovascular Care Transformation

ARPA-H program designed to produce the first FDA-authorized clinical agentic AI. Three Technical Areas: patient-facing CVD agent, disease-agnostic supervisory architecture, and health system integration.

Contractor note: Awards expected June 2026. The TA2 winner owns the oversight layer for every subsequent clinical agentic AI in healthcare.

Federal Health

ARPA-H

Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health

Federal R&D agency funding high-risk, high-reward biomedical and health innovation. Funds through Other Transaction Agreements rather than contracts, grants, or cooperative agreements.

Contractor note: Zeroed in the proposed FY2027 HHS budget but executing FY2026 appropriations regardless. The ADVOCATE program awards in June 2026.

Federal Health

ATO

Authority to Operate

The security approval required before deploying any system on a government network. No ATO, no deployment.

Contractor note: Plan 6-18 months for the process. Start early or your timeline is fiction.

C

CCN

Community Care Network

The contract vehicle through which VA pays private-sector providers for veteran care. The FY2027 Medical Community Care account is $56.2B (+17%) — about 44% of all VA care delivery.

Contractor note: CCN Next Gen (36C10G26R0003) introduces value-based payments and utilization management for the first time. Proposals received April 17, 2026; award TBD.

VA

CIO-SP3

Chief Information Officer -- Solutions and Partners 3

NITAAC's primary IT GWAC. $40B ceiling. Extended through April 2027 after CIO-SP4 was cancelled February 2026.

Contractor note: If you hold a CIO-SP3 seat, protect it. It is one of the few active large GWACs.

Contracting

CIO-SP4

Chief Information Officer -- Solutions and Partners 4

NITAAC's planned successor to CIO-SP3. Cancelled February 2026 after years of protests. The cancellation reshuffled the GWAC landscape.

Contractor note: Watch for rebid announcements. Firms that invested in this bid process face sunk costs with no vehicle.

Contracting

CMMC

Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification

Third-party cybersecurity certification becoming mandatory for DoD contractors. Level 2 is the new table stakes.

Contractor note: Get certified before the deadlines hit. This is not optional for DoD work.

Cybersecurity

D

Defense Health Networks

Regional Healthcare Delivery

DHA's regional management structure replacing individual Service medical commands.

Contractor note: Your regional POCs for implementation and sustainment contracts.

Defense Health

DHA

Defense Health Agency

Runs military healthcare operations, TRICARE, pharmacy, and health IT for 9.6M beneficiaries.

Contractor note: Your buyer for most DoD health IT work. DHA issues the task orders.

Defense Health

DHACA

DHA Contracting Activity

The contracting arm of DHA. Issues and manages health IT contract vehicles.

Contractor note: DHACA is where your proposals go. Know their evaluation preferences.

Defense Health

DoD Impact Levels

Cloud Security Classification

IL2 through IL6 classify what data DoD cloud systems can handle. IL4+ requires dedicated infrastructure.

Contractor note: Know your IL. It determines which cloud environments you can use and what data you can process.

Cybersecurity

E

EHRM

Electronic Health Record Modernization

VA's program to replace VistA with Oracle Health. Lifecycle cost estimates range from $37B to $49B. After Michigan's April 2026 go-live, fewer than one in six VA medical centers will be live.

Contractor note: Congress withheld 30% of FY2026 funding (~$1.02B) pending three June 1, 2026 conditions. The Oracle subcontractor and support ecosystem is the primary addressable market for most organizations.

VA

EHRM-IO

EHR Modernization Integration Office

VA office managing the Oracle Health EHR deployment.

Contractor note: EHRM-IO runs the deployment program. Know their priorities and pain points.

VA

F

FAR

Federal Acquisition Regulation

The primary rule book for government procurement. Applies to all federal contracts.

Contractor note: FAR knowledge is non-negotiable. Focus on Parts 8, 12, 15, and 16 for IT services.

Contracting

FedRAMP

Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program

Security certification required for any cloud product serving federal agencies.

Contractor note: FedRAMP authorization is table stakes for selling cloud to the government.

Cybersecurity

FEHRM

Federal Electronic Health Record Modernization

Joint office coordinating EHR interoperability between DoD and VA.

Contractor note: FEHRM sets interoperability standards that both DoD and VA contracts must follow.

Defense Health

FHIR R4

Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources

The modern healthcare data exchange standard mandated by the 21st Century Cures Act. RESTful APIs.

Contractor note: FHIR R4 support is a requirement in most federal health IT RFPs.

Interoperability

G

GWAC

Government-Wide Acquisition Contract

IT-specific contract vehicles that any federal agency can use. CIO-SP3, SEWP, Alliant 3 are the big ones.

Contractor note: GWAC access opens doors across all agencies, not just one. Worth the investment to get on one.

Contracting

H

HL7 v2

Health Level Seven Version 2

The legacy healthcare messaging standard. Still dominant in hospital interfaces despite FHIR.

Contractor note: Most existing systems speak HL7 v2. Your integration layer needs both HL7 v2 and FHIR.

Interoperability

I

IDIQ

Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity

Contract type with no guaranteed minimum. Government orders work via task orders over the contract period.

Contractor note: Most federal health IT is bought through IDIQs. Win the vehicle first, then compete for task orders.

Contracting

Information Blocking

21st Century Cures Act Provision

Federal prohibition on practices that unreasonably limit health information exchange. Applies to health IT developers.

Contractor note: Your software cannot restrict data sharing without qualifying for an exception. Violations carry penalties.

Interoperability

J

JLV

Joint Longitudinal Viewer

Read-only viewer that bridges DoD and VA patient records for continuity of care.

Contractor note: JLV integration is a common requirement in health IT RFPs.

Defense Health

M

MHS

Military Health System

The entire DoD healthcare enterprise: MTFs, TRICARE, dental, pharmacy, and all supporting IT systems.

Contractor note: Understanding MHS structure tells you where money flows and who has authority.

Defense Health

MHS EITS

MHS Enterprise IT Services

DHA's umbrella contract for enterprise IT infrastructure and managed services.

Contractor note: MHS EITS covers network, compute, and cloud for the entire Military Health System.

Contracting

MHS GENESIS

DoD Electronic Health Record

The single largest health IT deployment in federal history. Built on Oracle Health (Cerner), now live at 130+ facilities.

Contractor note: Integration, training, and sustainment opportunities surround this platform.

Defense Health

MQS2-NG

Medical Quality Systems Services Next Generation

DHA contract vehicle for medical quality, patient safety, and clinical decision support IT.

Contractor note: Niche but well-funded. Medical quality IT is a growing area.

Contracting

MTF

Military Treatment Facility

Hospitals and clinics on military installations. Each runs MHS GENESIS.

Contractor note: MTFs are where your technology gets deployed and tested.

Defense Health

N

NIST SP 800-171

Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information

110 security controls required for handling CUI in non-federal systems. CMMC is built on this.

Contractor note: If you handle CUI (most health IT does), you must implement these controls.

Cybersecurity

O

OASIS+

One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus

GSA's multi-agency professional services contract. Covers IT, management consulting, and more.

Contractor note: OASIS+ is the primary path for large professional services work across agencies.

Contracting

Oracle Health Federal EHR

Commercial EHR Platform

The commercial platform (formerly Cerner Millennium) underlying both MHS GENESIS and VA EHRM.

Contractor note: Oracle Health sets the platform constraints your solutions must integrate with.

VA

P

PEO-DHMS / OPMED

Program Executive Office, Defense Healthcare Management Systems

Now called OPMED. DoD's lead office for medical IT development and acquisition.

Contractor note: This is the acquisition authority for major health IT programs.

Defense Health

R

RMF

Risk Management Framework

NIST's structured approach to security assessment: categorize, select, implement, assess, authorize, monitor.

Contractor note: RMF is the process that gets you to ATO. Know the six steps cold.

Cybersecurity

S

SAM.gov

System for Award Management

The federal government's official system for entity registration, contract opportunities, and award data.

Contractor note: You cannot win a federal contract without SAM registration. Keep it current.

Contracting

SMART on FHIR

Substitutable Medical Applications, Reusable Technologies

App launch and authorization framework built on FHIR. Enables third-party apps on EHR platforms.

Contractor note: SMART on FHIR is how you build apps that plug into MHS GENESIS and VA systems.

Interoperability

T

T4NG

Transformation Twenty-One Total Technology Next Generation

VA's primary IT services IDIQ. $22.3B ceiling. Managed by VA TAC.

Contractor note: T4NG is how VA buys most of its IT services. Critical vehicle for VA health IT work.

Contracting

TEFCA

Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement

The national infrastructure for health data exchange. Connects all major health networks.

Contractor note: TEFCA compliance is increasingly required. Understand the Qualified Health Information Networks.

Interoperability

TRICARE

Military Health Insurance

Health insurance for 9.6M service members, retirees, and families. Managed by DHA.

Contractor note: TRICARE IT systems (claims, enrollment, referrals) are major contract areas.

Defense Health

U

USCDI

United States Core Data for Interoperability

The standardized set of health data classes systems must exchange. Updated annually by ONC.

Contractor note: USCDI defines the minimum data your system must handle. Check the latest version.

Interoperability

V

VA EHRM

VA EHR Modernization

VA's program to replace VistA with Oracle Health (same platform as MHS GENESIS). Michigan four-site go-live in April 2026 — first multi-site simultaneous deployment in program history.

Contractor note: Congress withheld $1.02B pending June 1, 2026 conditions. Status changes here shift billions in contract opportunities.

VA

VBP

Value-Based Payments

Payment models that tie reimbursement to outcomes instead of services rendered. Enter VA community care for the first time under CCN Next Gen.

Contractor note: Future VBP model designs will be proposed by TOPR awardees. The organization that proposes the next cardiac or oncology episode model shapes the contract's economics.

VA

VistA

Veterans Health Information Systems and Technology Architecture

VA's legacy EHR built in-house since the 1970s. Still running at most VA facilities.

Contractor note: VistA sustainment, migration, and modernization are active contract areas.

VA

Z

Zero Trust Architecture

Security Model

DoD's mandated security architecture. Never trust, always verify. Continuous authentication and authorization.

Contractor note: Every new DoD IT system must implement zero trust principles. Build it in from day one.
