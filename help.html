Using ProposalPulse

ProposalPulse scores your federal proposal draft against 9 criteria that mirror how government evaluators read Section L/M responses.

How to use it

  1. Go to missionmeetstech.com/proposal-pulse
  2. Upload your document (PDF, DOCX, or PPTX — max 4MB)
  3. Select the document type
  4. Enter your email address
  5. Click "Score this document"
  6. Scorecard arrives by email in 30-90 seconds

Scoring criteria

  1. Problem Clarity
  2. Mission Relevance
  3. Technical Approach Clarity
  4. Vehicle & Acquisition Fit
  5. Team and Credibility
  6. Traction
  7. Cost / Price Credibility
  8. Competitive Position
  9. Funding Ask & Scope

Pricing: First assessment free. Additional: $19.99 each ($14.99 for Premium members).

Privacy: Original files are not stored. Extracted text may be retained for up to 90 days to support score retrieval and follow-up review. Scorecards and related assessment outputs may be retained. Data is processed by named service providers and is not used to train models. Do not upload classified, ITAR/EAR, or otherwise restricted government material.

Using MarketPulse

MarketPulse turns a federal health IT market question into a source-cited intelligence brief delivered within 24 hours.

  1. Go to missionmeetstech.com/marketpulse
  2. Enter your question
  3. Enter your email address
  4. Click "Request brief"
  5. Brief arrives by email within 24 hours

Pricing: First brief free. Additional: $50 each ($35 for Premium members).

Is the newsletter free?

Yes. Analysis every Tuesday and Friday, the full article archive, and the podcast are all free.

A monthly brief covering programs, vehicles, and budget signals. Each signal includes an action window and confidence label.

Premium subscribers submit up to 2 questions per month through the Ask MMT portal. Answers are published editorially so every subscriber benefits.
