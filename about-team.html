The Team

The people behind Mission Meets Tech. Independent analysts delivering federal health IT intelligence for the operators and executives who need it most.

Mary Womack

Founder, Mission Meets Tech

Mary Womack is an analyst, strategist, and independent voice working at the intersection of federal health policy, defense acquisition, and technology delivery. With a career spanning DoD, DHA, VA, and civilian agencies, she translates the distance between policy announcements and operational outcomes into intelligence that decision-makers can actually use.

Mary is known for her ability to cut through vendor narrative and surface what's real — identifying where modernization initiatives stall, where contract signals matter, and what leadership changes mean for program delivery.

Her work focuses on the defense health ecosystem: tracking how decisions made inside DHA, VA, and Congress ripple through contractors, clinicians, and the service members who depend on these systems working. The filter is always the same — does this save lives or enhance readiness?

Through Mission Meets Tech, Mary provides independent, evidence-based analysis for the people who build, buy, and operate federal health systems — because they deserve intelligence that tells the truth, not just what sells.
